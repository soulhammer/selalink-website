Cherished for their brilliant red coloration against green aquatic plants, the Cherry Shrimp (Neocaridina davidi) is an iconic freshwater dwarf shrimp widely kept as an algae grazer and cleanup crew. While relatively hardy, their crustacean nature requires careful mineral management (GH, KH, TDS). Sudden water parameter shifts or calcium/magnesium deficits cause the fatal 'White Ring of Death' (molting failure). Furthermore, they are hypersensitive to copper and heavy metals—medications containing copper can wipe out an entire colony within minutes. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your shrimp's physical signals and maintain pristine water parameters.
