Have you ever bought a whole box of sweet tangerines only to find the bottom layers crushed, mushy, and covered in green mold? 🍊

Tangerines have high moisture content and thin peels, making them prone to crushing, humidity trapped between fruits, and rapid decay. In particular, microscopic mold spores on the peel proliferate explosively when exposed to moisture and ambient warmth.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step tangerine storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to keep your citrus sweet, firm, and mold-free. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Wash in diluted saltwater and dry thoroughly — Mold Spore Removal & Drying 💧</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Washing away microscopic mold spores and impurities from the peel dramatically extends shelf life. 💡 Storage Tip: Soak tangerines in mild saltwater (or baking soda water) for 1–2 minutes, then dry each fruit completely with paper towels or a dry cloth.
  </p>
</div>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Layer with newspaper or paper towels — Moisture Control & Cushioning 📰</h4>

  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Discard moldy tangerines immediately and wash surrounding ones 🍊</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      When blue or green mold forms on a tangerine's surface, spores rapidly spread to neighboring fruits. Discard moldy tangerines immediately, separate adjacent fruit, wash them thoroughly, dry them completely, and consume them first.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not stack tangerines too deep in multiple layers 📦</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Stacking tangerines in deep boxes crushes the bottom fruits under weight, causing them to burst and leak juice. Trapped moisture spreads throughout the box, serving as the primary trigger for mold proliferation.
    </p>
