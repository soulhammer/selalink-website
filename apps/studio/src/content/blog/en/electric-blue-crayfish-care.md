The Electric Blue Crayfish (Procambarus alleni) is a stunning freshwater crustacean famous for its vibrant cobalt blue coloration. Reaching 10 to 15 cm as adults, they feature powerful claws and engaging behaviors. However, like all crustaceans, they undergo periodic molting (ecdysis) to grow. Immediately after molting, their soft exoskeleton leaves them extremely vulnerable to predation and cannibalism. Providing adequate calcium/magnesium minerals for shell hardening and avoiding cohabitation with slow bottom fish or dwarf shrimp is vital. In this guide, outlines Electric Blue Crayfish behavioral signals and shares a 3-step husbandry routine.
