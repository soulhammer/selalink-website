The African Grey Parrot is widely recognized as one of the most cognitively advanced birds, demonstrating problem-solving skills and vocabulary retention comparable to a 4-to-5-year-old human child. Beyond mere vocal mimicry, they can comprehend context and communicate meaningfully. However, their high intelligence makes them emotionally sensitive. Without adequate cognitive stimulation and environmental enrichment, boredom and chronic stress often manifest as Feather Damaging Behavior (FDB) or self-mutilation. Today, PetSelf outlines African Grey behavioral cues and shares a 3-step mental enrichment routine to safeguard their psychological and physical well-being.
