Renowned for curling into a tight ball with its head tucked safely inside, the Ball Python (Python regius) is one of the most popular pet snakes worldwide thanks to its gentle nature and diverse color morphs. As ectothermic reptiles, Ball Pythons require a clear thermal gradient between a cool zone (24°C–26°C) and a hot/basking zone (31°C–33°C). Ambient humidity should be maintained at 55%–60% normally, increasing to 70%–80% during shedding cycles to prevent dysecdysis (incomplete shed). Cold temperatures frequently trigger acute Respiratory Infections (RI) and hunger strikes. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your snake's signals and maintain its health.
