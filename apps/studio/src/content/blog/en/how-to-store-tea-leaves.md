Have you ever bought aromatic tea leaves only to find their delicate flavor faded, stale, or flat after a few weeks? 🍃

Although loose tea leaves are completely dry, they act like a sponge, rapidly absorbing oxygen, moisture, light, heat, and surrounding odors. Exposure to humidity and oxygen oxidizes beneficial compounds like catechins and theanine, destroying freshness and leaving behind a dull, bitter taste.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step tea storage guide recommended by USDA and tea experts to preserve subtle aromas and vibrant flavor for months. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not store tea in transparent glass jars or clear plastic ☀️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      UV rays and light rapidly break down chlorophyll and volatile aroma compounds, changing the tea color and stripping away its sweet, floral notes. Always use opaque, light-blocking containers.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">

  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Keep tea away from spices and refrigerator odors 👃</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Tea leaves have a porous microscopic structure that instantly absorbs volatile odor molecules (coffee, garlic, spices, or fridge odors). Store tea strictly separated from strong-smelling items.
    </p>
