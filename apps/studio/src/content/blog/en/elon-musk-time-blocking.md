## 1. Parkinson's Law and the Brain's Tunneling Mechanism

According to Parkinson's Law, if the time given for a task expands, the cognitive intensity loosens, consuming all the given time. Restricting time extremely triggers the 'tunneling effect' that blocks distractions by releasing adrenaline and dopamine. Musk's micro-blocking guides the brain to focus on the deadline of each short block, preventing decision fatigue and raising cognitive production density per hour to the limit.
