Have you ever stored pistachios in your pantry only to find them stale, soft, or smelling rancid after a short time? 🥜

Pistachios are rich in unsaturated fatty acids, which rapidly undergo lipid peroxidation upon contact with atmospheric oxygen, leading to offensive rancid odors. More critically, when relative humidity rises to $RH \ge 65\%$, the mold *Aspergillus* thrives and produces Aflatoxin—a potent Group 1 human carcinogen. Therefore, controlling moisture and oxygen is vital.

In this guide, we share a scientifically proven 3-step pistachio storage guide based on standards from the FDA, USDA, and MFDS to safely preserve their crunchy texture and rich flavor. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Seal tightly and store in a cool, dry pantry — Room Temp Storage (90 Days) 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    If you plan to consume pistachios within a few months, store them at a cool room temperature. 💡 Storage Tip: Place pistachios in an airtight container or ziplock bag and store in a dark, cool cabinet (15–20°C / 59–68°F, $RH \le 60\%$) away from sunlight to maintain crispness for 3 months.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Refrigerate in airtight containers for medium-term storage — Cold Storage (180 Days) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    During hot summer months or for storage beyond 3 months, low temperatures are significantly safer. 💡 Storage Tip: Refrigerate pistachios at 0–5°C (32–41°F) in an airtight container. Cold conditions slow lipid oxidation rates, preserving nutty flavor and crunch for 6 months.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Freeze in double-sealed bags for long-term preservation — Frozen Storage (360+ Days) 🧊</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Freezing is the best choice for bulk purchases or storage over 6 months. 💡 Storage Tip: Divide into smaller portions, seal tightly in double freezer bags, and store below -18°C (0°F). This completely suppresses lipid oxidation and mold growth, keeping pistachios fresh for over a year.
  </p>
</div>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions
