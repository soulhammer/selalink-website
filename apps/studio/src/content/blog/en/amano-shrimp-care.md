Renowned as the premier algae cleanup crew in planted aquariums, the Amano Shrimp (Caridina multidentata) is a translucent freshwater crustacean with dark lateral dots. While they tirelessly consume hair and thread algae, they are acutely sensitive to heavy metals (especially copper), elevated nitrates, and sudden shifts in water parameters or temperature. Poor water quality often triggers desperate tank escapes or fatal molting complications. In this guide, details a 3-step daily routine to safeguard your Amano Shrimp.
