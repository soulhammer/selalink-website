Have you ever opened a jar of peanut butter only to find an oily layer floating on top or a stale, rancid smell instead of a rich, nutty aroma? 🥜

Peanut butter is high in unsaturated fatty acids, making it susceptible to lipid oxidation (rancidity) when exposed to air, light, or heat. Natural peanut butter without emulsifiers is particularly prone to oil separation and rapid quality loss, making proper storage essential.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step peanut butter storage guide based on safety guidelines from the USDA, FDA, and MFDS to keep your peanut butter fresh and flavorful. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never use a wet or contaminated utensil ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Inserting a wet spoon or one with breadcrumbs introduces moisture, saliva, and contaminants into the jar, creating an ideal environment for mold and bacteria to thrive. Always use a clean, completely dry spoon.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Avoid direct sunlight and heat sources ☀️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Exposure to heat and light accelerates the oxidation of unsaturated fatty acids in peanut butter, causing rancidity and off-odors. Store it in a cool, dark pantry or refrigerator away from stoves and direct light.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
