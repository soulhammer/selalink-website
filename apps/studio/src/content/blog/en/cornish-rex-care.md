The Cornish Rex is an enchanting breed often dubbed the 'pixie of the cat fancy' thanks to its tight, marcel-waved coat, large bat-like ears, and slender greyhound-like silhouette. Because they possess only a soft down undercoat with no guard hairs, shedding is minimal. However, without outer fur to absorb natural skin sebum, oils quickly accumulate on their skin fold, ears, and paw pads. Additionally, their minimal body fat makes them highly sensitive to cold temperatures, while their boundless, kitten-like energy drives them to perform high-flying acrobatic jumps. In this guide, decodes Cornish Rex behavioral signals and presents a 3-step daily homecare routine for skin oil hygiene, thermal warmth, and high-energy vertical enrichment.
