Karl Marx dissected the economic structures of capitalism. How did his masterpiece Das Kapital come to life? 🏛️

The academic foundation of Marx was the British Museum Reading Room in London. During his exile, for about 30 years, he arrived at 9 AM when the reading room opened and stayed until 7 PM, spending over 10 hours daily immersed in texts. Under the giant dome, at his fixed seat, Marx examined vast data like rail statistics and agricultural ledgers. He copied key parts and added interpretations in his notes. This persistent study routine left a huge footprint on human thought.

Today, BuildSelf explores the neuroscientific meaning of Marx's reading routine and proposes a practical analog deep reading routine for modern people to cut through information noise.

## 1. Structuring the Association Cortex to Connect the Essence of Information

Skimming online articles stimulates only the visual cortex, but deep reading and cross-referencing activate the brain's <strong>association cortex</strong>. The association cortex acts as an integration hub, synthesizing sensory, linguistic, and reasoning inputs into conceptual frameworks. Meticulously extracting data from documents and analyzing it, as Marx did, optimizes synaptic connectivity. This allows the brain to escape fragmented thinking and construct macro-systemic models.
