Cherished for generations for their enchanting song and vibrant plumage, the Canary (Serinus canaria domestica) is a quintessential songbird. Unlike parrots, canaries value their independent space over physical handling. However, due to their delicate avian air sac respiratory system, they are hypersensitive to airborne toxins like Teflon (PTFE) fumes and scented sprays. Furthermore, irregular photoperiods disrupt hormone balance, leading to severe molting stress. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your canary's health signals and protect their breathing and plumage.
