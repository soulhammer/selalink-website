The Yorkshire Terrier, affectionately known as the 'Yorkie', packs a bold and spirited personality inside a toy-sized body. Possessing a fine, silky single coat remarkably similar in structure to human hair, their coat requires delicate daily care to prevent tangling. 🐩 Additionally, as a toy breed, Yorkies are highly susceptible to orthopedic issues, particularly patellar luxation (dislocated kneecaps). Today, PetSelf decodes Yorkie body language and outlines a 3-step homecare routine to maintain their glorious coat and strengthen their knee joints.
