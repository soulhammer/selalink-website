Have you ever put sesame oil in the refrigerator to keep it fresh, only to find it frozen solid into a cloudy white mass with faded aroma? 🫗

Unlike perilla oil, sesame oil contains powerful natural antioxidants called lignans (such as sesamol and sesamolin), making it highly resistant to lipid oxidation (rancidity). However, storing sesame oil in cold environments below 10°C (such as a refrigerator) causes it to solidify into white crystals, which impairs its rich aromatic oil compounds. Conversely, exposure to direct sunlight or heat near stovetops causes rapid oxidation.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide recommended by food safety experts to preserve the nutty aroma, rich flavor, and nutrients of your sesame oil for up to 1 to 2 years. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never store sesame oil in the refrigerator ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Sesame oil begins to solidify (freeze) at temperatures below 10–15°C (50–59°F). Refrigerating it makes it hard and inconvenient to use, while repeated freezing and thawing destroys its rich aromatic compounds. Always store at cool room temperature.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not keep in transparent bottles exposed to sunlight ☀️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Ultraviolet (UV) rays and direct light are major triggers for lipid photo-oxidation. Use dark brown glass bottles or wrap clear bottles with aluminum foil or paper to completely block light.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
