Do you leave healthy daily nuts like almonds, walnuts, and pecans out on the dining table in clear glass jars? 🥜

Nuts consist of 50–70% healthy unsaturated fatty acids, making them extremely susceptible to rapid lipid oxidation (rancidity) when exposed to heat, light, and oxygen. Under warm, humid room-temperature conditions, Aspergillus mold can grow and produce 'aflatoxin', a potent Class 1 carcinogen.

Today, FreshSnap shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on guidelines from the FDA, USDA, and MFDS to completely block mold toxins and preserve the rich flavor and crisp crunch of your nuts for over 6 months. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Seal tightly and store in the fridge or freezer once opened — Cold Storage (90 Days Refrigerated, 180 Days Frozen) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Leaving nuts at warm room temperatures causes unsaturated fats to oxidize quickly, releasing offensive odors and risking aflatoxin contamination. 💡 Storage Tip: Seal opened nuts in airtight containers and store in the fridge (0–4°C) for up to 3 months, or in the freezer (-18°C) for over 6 months.
  </p>
</div>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Portion into opaque airtight containers or aluminum Ziploc bags — Block Light & Oxygen 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Ultraviolet light and oxygen are primary drivers of lipid rancidity in nuts. 💡 Storage Tip: Instead of clear glass jars, portion nuts into light-blocking opaque containers or aluminum foil Ziploc bags. Adding a food-grade silica gel pack absorbs lingering moisture and oxygen.
  </p>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Never eat nuts that smell rancid or feel stale and soft ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      A stale oil odor or limp texture indicates advanced lipid oxidation and potential aflatoxin mold growth. Aflatoxins are heat-resistant toxins that cannot be destroyed by washing or roasting, so discard spoiled nuts immediately.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Handle nut kernels carefully to avoid surface damage 🥜</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Crushing or damaging the outer skin of walnuts or almonds increases surface area exposure to oxygen, accelerating oxidation dozens of times over. Avoid squeezing or crushing nut packaging.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
