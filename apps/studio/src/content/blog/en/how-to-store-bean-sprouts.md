Have you ever bought a bag of crisp soybean or mung bean sprouts, put them straight into the fridge, only to find the stems turning black, slimy, and spoiled a few days later? 🌱

Bean sprouts consist of over 90% water and remain highly active in respiration even after harvesting. When trapped inside a plastic bag, moisture gets locked in while oxygen is restricted, creating a humid environment that exponentially speeds up bacterial growth and decay.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to keep your bean sprouts firm, crisp, and fresh. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Sort damaged stems and rinse gently — Remove Decay Sources 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Blackened or mushy stems accelerate decay in surrounding healthy sprouts. 💡 Storage Tip: Pick out bruised stems, gently rinse under cold running water to remove loose hulls and debris.
  </p>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Submerge in cold water inside an airtight container — Moisture Preservation 🌊</h4>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Refrigerate at 3–5°C and replace water daily — Cold Storage (7–10 Days) ❄️</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never leave sprouts sealed in their store bag ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Leaving sprouts in their original plastic bag traps respiration moisture, causing rapid browning, mushiness, and bad odors. Remove them from the packaging immediately after purchase.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">

    </p>
