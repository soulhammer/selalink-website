Named 'Lovebirds' for the intense bonds they form with their companions, Lovebirds (Agapornis) are cherished worldwide for their striking colors and vibrant personalities. However, their strong bonding nature comes with high territoriality and hormonal sensitivity. Long daylight hours or excessive petting on the back can trigger chronic egg-laying and fatal Egg Binding in females. Furthermore, their air sac respiratory system makes them extremely vulnerable to airborne toxins like PTFE (Teflon) fumes and perfumes, while an all-seed diet causes Vitamin A deficiency and fatty liver disease. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Lovebird's body signals and maintain its health.
