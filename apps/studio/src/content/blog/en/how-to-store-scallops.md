Have you ever purchased sweet, delicate scallops only to find them smelling fishy and losing their firm texture after a short time in the fridge? 🦪

Scallop adductor muscles are high in glycogen and have delicate protein tissue, making them highly susceptible to rapid post-mortem autolysis by endogenous enzymes. Additionally, Trimethylamine N-oxide (TMAO) present in scallops is reduced into Trimethylamine (TMA) by spoilage bacteria, generating an offensive fishy odor. Therefore, ultra-low temperature storage at 0–2°C is essential.

In this guide, we share a scientifically proven 3-step scallop storage guide based on guidelines from NOAA, USDA, and MFDS to preserve their natural sweetness and plump texture. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Eviscerate, rinse with salt water, and store ultra-cold — Refrigerated Storage (1–2 Days) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Fresh raw scallops should be eviscerated immediately, washed gently in cold light salt water ( ~ 3% salinity), and thoroughly patted dry. 💡 Storage Tip: Line an airtight container with paper towels, lay the scallops flat, and store them in the coldest compartment (0–2°C / 32–35°F). Consume within 1 to 2 days.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Flash freeze to halt autolysis and tissue degradation — Frozen Storage (90–180 Days) 🧊</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    If storing for more than 2 days, freeze scallops immediately to halt autolytic enzyme activity and TMA formation. 💡 Storage Tip: Dry scallop meats completely, wrap tightly in vacuum or plastic film in single-meal portions, and freeze below -18°C (0°F). Maintains texture for 3 to 6 months.
  </p>
</div>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Thaw slowly in the fridge or cold saline to prevent drip loss — Scientific Thawing 🧪</h4>
  </div>

  </p>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Prevent rapid autolysis and microbial TMA odor formation ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Enzymatic autolysis rapidly degrades scallop muscle protein after harvest. Microbial breakdown of TMAO into TMA accelerates spoilage and creates harsh fishy smells, making strict temperature control at 0–2°C mandatory.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Avoid soaking in fresh tap water or leaving viscera attached 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Soaking scallops in fresh tap water disrupts osmotic balance, causing cells to swell and leak sweet flavor compounds. Always rinse gently with cold saline water and promptly remove internal organs before storing.
    </p>
