Erwin Schrödinger, the genius Austrian physicist who won the Nobel Prize in Physics and laid the foundation for modern quantum mechanics by discovering the wave equation, the most mysterious and complex physical law in history. In what environment did his brilliant mathematical calculations and deep physical-philosophical thoughts come from? 🌌

Whenever Schrödinger went into highly complex calculations and research, he always inserted earplugs deeply into both ears to maintain absolute auditory silence. Even in the noiseless environment of the mountain sanatorium where he stayed for tuberculosis treatment, he believed that even natural sounds like the wind or birds chirping wasted the brain's precious energy and distracted attention. Only after securing a state of complete silence (White Silence) could he immerse himself in advanced calculations of theoretical physics. For Schrödinger, physical earplugs were the key to bonding his brain to pure logic without sensory interference.

## 1. Silence Effect to Prevent Energy Consumption of Sensory Filtering

Human hearing is the most sensitive sensory organ that induces arousal of the brain to detect threats, operating even while sleeping. Whenever minor environmental noise is heard, the brain unconsciously activates sensory filtering to check if this noise is safe, consuming the cognitive energy (attention resources) of the prefrontal cortex. When auditory input is completely cut off using earplugs, the brain stops consuming oxygen and glucose wasted on filtering unnecessary sensory data. This allows all conserved energy to be 100% directed only to the task at hand, reducing cognitive fatigue and maximizing focus.

## 3. Preventing Tinnitus and Adhering to Ear Hygiene
<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-indigo-500 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">💡</span>
  <div>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      If you wear earplugs too deeply for too long, the pressure inside the ear can change, causing tinnitus, or moisture can build up, leading to ear diseases like otitis externa. Limit focus sessions to a maximum of 50 minutes, and always remove earplugs during breaks to dry them and let your ears breathe. Keep earplugs clean and replace them regularly to ensure hygiene.
    </p>
