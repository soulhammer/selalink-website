The Oriental Shorthair is a sleek, modern breed derived from Siamese bloodlines, renowned for its large bat-like ears, elegant wedge-shaped head, and slender body. Exceptionally vocal and intelligent, they actively engage in conversation with their human companions, seeking constant affection and human contact. However, because they possess a fine, short coat with virtually no undercoat, they are highly sensitive to cold environments and vulnerable to respiratory chills if room temperatures drop. Furthermore, due to their intense sociability, they suffer from isolation distress if left alone for extended periods. Providing interactive play, dedicated thermal care, and responsive communication is crucial for their well-being. Today on PetSelf, we detail Oriental Shorthair vocal signals, thermal management, and a 3-step daily care routine.
