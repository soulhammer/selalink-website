Labrador Retrievers are beloved worldwide for their gentle, intelligent, and friendly nature. However, a significant portion of Labs possess a POMC gene mutation that affects satiety, making them constantly hungry and prone to obesity—which severely impacts their joints and increases the risk of hip dysplasia. Additionally, while they love water, their folded ears trap moisture after swimming, making them vulnerable to ear infections. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to keep your Lab healthy, active, and thriving.
