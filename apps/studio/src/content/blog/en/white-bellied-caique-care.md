The White-bellied Caique (Pionites leucogaster) is a vibrant medium-sized South American parrot famous for its striking plumage, white chest, and unique habit of hopping on two feet like a bunny—earning it the title 'The Beagle of the Parrot World.' Highly energetic and affection-seeking, caiques love interactive wrestling play and acrobatics, making them top favorites among parrot enthusiasts worldwide. However, their intense curiosity and powerful chewing drive demand a continuous supply of foraging and destruction toys; without mental stimulation, they can develop destructive behaviors, excessive screaming, or feather plucking. Furthermore, maintaining a strict 10–12 hour dark sleep routine is critical to prevent hormonal aggression. In this guide, we outline caique behavior signals, enrichment setups, and a 3-step daily care routine.
