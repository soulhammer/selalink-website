With their smiling expressions and distinctive spotted tails, Leopard Geckos are the premier choice for reptile companion keepers. According to ARAV guidelines, as ectothermic creatures dependent on external heat sources, establishing a precise thermal gradient (Hot Zone 30°C ~ 32°C / Cool Zone 24°C ~ 26°C) and maintaining a humid hide (Wet Cave) is fundamental to their survival.
