The Blue-tongued Skink (Tiliqua spp.) is a diurnal, terrestrial lizard native to Australia and Indonesia, famous for its vibrant blue tongue displayed when startled and its signature stocky body with short legs. Renowned in the reptile-keeping world for its dog-like docility and high intelligence, it is widely considered the ultimate medium-to-large starter lizard. However, because they are opportunistic omnivores in the wild, failing to balance their protein and vegetable ratios can quickly lead to obesity, vitamin deficiencies, or Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD). Additionally, maintaining a distinct thermal gradient with a hot basking zone and a cool side, along with providing a deep burrowing substrate, is vital for their physical and mental well-being. Today on PetSelf, we detail Blue-tongued Skink defense behaviors, omnivorous dietary guidelines, and a 3-step care routine.
