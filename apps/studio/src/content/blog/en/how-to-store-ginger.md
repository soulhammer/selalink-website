Have you ever bought fresh ginger only to find it covered in mold or dried up like a piece of wood after a few days? 🫚

Ginger is rich in moisture and fiber, but it is extremely sensitive to humidity, which quickly causes mold. Conversely, prolonged exposure to dry air evaporates its internal moisture, leaving it tough and fibrous. Moreover, moldy ginger develops dangerous mycotoxins, requiring careful storage practices.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step ginger storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to preserve peak freshness and flavor. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Brush off soil and wrap in paper towels — Moisture Control (1–2 Weeks) 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Managing moisture while preventing dehydration is key. 💡 Storage Tip: Lightly brush off loose soil from unwashed ginger, wrap each piece tightly in paper towels or newspaper, and store at cool room temperature (10–15°C) or in the refrigerator crisper drawer.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Do not wash ginger before storing 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Dry soil on unwashed ginger acts as a natural protective barrier. Washing ginger allows moisture to penetrate skin crevices, creating an ideal environment for mold and bacterial growth. Simply brush off excess soil and wash right before cooking.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Never consume ginger that has any mold on it ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      When ginger rots or molds, harmful toxins such as safrole spread throughout the root. Even if you cut away the moldy portion, invisible mycotoxins penetrate deep into the fibrous tissue. Always discard the entire root if mold appears.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
