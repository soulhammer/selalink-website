With their plush double coat and signature round, flat faces, Persian cats are beloved indoor companions known for their gentle and calm nature. However, their brachycephalic facial structure causes tears to overflow constantly, easily trapping debris in their skin folds. Furthermore, their dense, fine longhair coat readily tangles into solid mats, pulling on the skin to cause severe pain and blocking air circulation, which triggers dermatitis. In this guide, decodes Persian cat eye and coat stress signals and provides a 3-step homecare routine to prevent these issues.
