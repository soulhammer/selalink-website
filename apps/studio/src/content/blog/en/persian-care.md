With their thick coat and flat face, Persian cats are known for their gentle and calm nature. However, their flat facial structure causes excessive tearing, which can lead to bacterial infections in their skin folds. Their dense longhair coat easily tangles, causing pain and stopping air circulation. Today, PetSelf decodes Persian cat eye and coat stress signals and provides a 3-step homecare routine.
