Have you ever left fresh shrimp in the refrigerator only to find its head and legs turning black (melanosis) accompanied by a strong fishy odor? 🦐

Shrimp high in protein and moisture content undergoes rapid enzymatic breakdown and bacterial deterioration immediately after harvest. When exposed to atmospheric oxygen, tyrosinase enzymes trigger melanosis (black spot formation) starting from the head, while microorganisms multiply quickly.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step shrimp storage guide recommended by seafood safety experts to preserve maximum freshness and texture. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Rinse in mild saltwater and pat completely dry — Flavor & Freshness Preservation 📦</h4>

  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">

  </p>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not soak shrimp in tap water (freshwater) for a long time 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Soaking shrimp in tap water causes osmotic loss of its natural savory flavor and moisture, rendering the meat mushy. Always rinse gently in mild saltwater ( ~ 3% salinity) to retain firmness and flavor.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not expose peeled raw shrimp meat directly to air ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Peeled raw shrimp meat oxidizes rapidly upon oxygen contact and loses moisture, leading to quick spoilage. Store shrimp with shells intact, or if peeled, submerge in airtight containers to minimize exposure to air.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
