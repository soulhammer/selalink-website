The Sulphur-crested Cockatoo is a dream pet bird renowned worldwide for its striking bright yellow crest, snow-white plumage, and exceptional emotional depth. Possessing toddler-like intelligence and intense social needs, they form profound emotional bonds with their human companions. However, their high dependency makes them vulnerable to distress, screeching, and feather-plucking (self-mutilation) if neglected. Additionally, they produce a fine white powder down for feather maintenance, requiring strict air quality control and frequent bathing. Today, PetSelf decodes cockatoo crest language and presents a 3-step daily care routine for feather hygiene and mental enrichment.
