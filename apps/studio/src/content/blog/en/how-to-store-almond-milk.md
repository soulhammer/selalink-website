Have you ever enjoyed a glass of nutty, refreshing almond milk only to find it curdling or smelling sour after a few days on the fridge door? 🥛

Almond milk is a plant-based beverage formed by suspending almond particles in water. Unlike fermented dairy products, opened almond milk is highly susceptible to microbial contamination from ambient air. Its high moisture content means leaving it at room temperature or storing it in the temperature-fluctuating fridge door rapidly speeds up bacterial growth and emulsion breakdown.

In this guide, shares a scientifically proven 3-step almond milk storage guide based on guidelines from the FDA and USDA to prevent separation and keep your almond milk safely fresh. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never leave at room temperature after opening 🚫</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      While unopened shelf-stable cartons can sit at room temperature, opening them introduces airborne microbes. Given its high moisture content, bacteria multiply exponentially within hours at room temperature, so refrigerate immediately after opening.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Store on an inside fridge shelf, not on the door ❄️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The refrigerator door experiences frequent temperature fluctuations whenever opened, encouraging bacterial blooms and fat separation. Always store opened almond milk on an interior shelf maintained at 0–4°C.
    </p>
  </div>
