Loved worldwide for their adorable cheek pouches, Dwarf Hamsters encompass species like Winter Whites, Campbell's, and Roborovskis. Dwarf Hamsters are genetically highly prone to Diabetes Mellitus if fed sugary foods like sugar beets or fruits. Furthermore, if room temperatures drop below 15°C (59°F), they can fall into a life-threatening state of pseudo-hibernation (Torpor / hypothermia). Water baths must be strictly avoided as they strip protective coat oils and induce fatal hypothermia, making sand baths essential. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to understand your dwarf hamster's body signals and maintain its health.
