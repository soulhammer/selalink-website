Cherished for centuries as royal lapdogs, the Shih Tzu is an iconic toy breed recognized by its flowing double coat and shortened snout. Due to their flat-faced brachycephalic anatomy, Shih Tzus struggle to regulate body temperature in hot or humid weather, making them exceptionally vulnerable to heatstroke and airway obstruction. Their prominent, large eyes are highly susceptible to corneal ulcers, dry eye (KCS), and tear staining, while their heavy drop ears predispose them to chronic ear infections. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Shih Tzu's health signals and protect its well-being.
