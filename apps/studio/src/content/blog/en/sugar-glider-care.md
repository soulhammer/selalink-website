Famous for gliding through the air with their patagium, Sugar Gliders (Petaurus breviceps) are small arboreal marsupials cherished worldwide. Possessing intense social instincts from their wild colony life, housing a single glider often leads to severe depression and self-mutilation. Furthermore, an improper diet lacking balanced calcium and phosphorus (2:1 ratio) triggers Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD), causing fatal hind-limb paralysis. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Sugar Glider's body signals, prevent paralysis, and maintain mental well-being.
