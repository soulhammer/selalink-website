The English Bulldog is an iconic medium-sized breed cherished globally for its deep facial wrinkles, sturdy stocky build, and surprisingly gentle demeanor. Featuring a much broader chest and heavier bone structure than the French Bulldog, this traditional breed possesses a short, brachycephalic muzzle that makes thermal regulation difficult. Accumulated moisture and debris within their facial folds and tail pocket can rapidly lead to bacterial or yeast intertrigo (skin fold dermatitis). In this guide, breaks down English Bulldog communication signals and outlines a 3-step homecare routine for wrinkle hygiene and heatstroke prevention.
