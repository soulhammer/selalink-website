Hailing as Russia's national treasure, the Siberian Cat is a magnificent large breed famed for its dense triple coat, dog-like devotion, and high intelligence. Siberians naturally produce lower levels of the Fel d 1 protein—the primary allergen in cats—making them popular among allergy sufferers, though they are not 100% allergen-free. As a slow-maturing breed taking 3 to 5 years to reach full size, they require tailored care to support their growth, manage their thick fur against hairballs, and monitor for genetic Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM). Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily routine to read your Siberian's body language and protect its health.
