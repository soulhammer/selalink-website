The Western Hognose Snake (Heterodon nasicus) is globally famous for its upturned snout and dramatic defensive behavior, earning its place among the 'top 3 pet snakes' alongside Corn Snakes and Ball Pythons. When threatened, they engage in theatrical bluffs like flattening their necks like a cobra or rolling over to play dead (thanatosis). Because they are natural burrowers, providing a deep layer of aspen bedding that holds tunnel shapes is essential. Establishing a precise thermal gradient with hot and cool zones is equally critical for their digestion and immunity. Today on PetSelf, we analyze the Western Hognose's defensive signals and share a 3-step homecare routine for substrate management, heating, and safe feeding.
