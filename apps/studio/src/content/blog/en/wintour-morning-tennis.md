Anna Wintour, the legendary editor-in-chief of Vogue who inspired the movie 'The Devil Wears Prada.' Where does her razor-sharp judgment behind her signature sunglasses and her tireless 24-hour energy come from? 🎾

Even in her seventies, Anna Wintour wakes up at exactly 5:00 a.m. every morning and grabs a tennis racket. Before checking business calls or emails, she rushes to the court to play a high-intensity tennis match for an hour. Only after sweating profusely and driving her blood circulation to its peak does she put on her sunglasses and enter the office. Her unshakeable composure and focus in the face of daily decision-making storms are the direct results of this physical discipline.

Today, BuildSelf demonstrates the neurobiological benefits of Wintour's morning tennis habit and provides a morning physical reset routine for busy modern people.

## 1. Body Temperature Elevation and Neuro-Awakening Secretion

Right after waking up in the morning, the brain experiences a period of cognitive grogginess as it transitions from sleep. If you face stressful meetings or emails without awakening the brain first, frontal lobe function gets impaired, making you prone to emotional decisions.

Performing intense cardio like tennis in the morning instantly floods the brain cells with blood flow and elevates body temperature. This stimulus boosts the secretion of <strong>'dopamine,'</strong> which accelerates executive function, and norepinephrine, which aids alertness. As a result, it dampens the overactivity of the amygdala, building a physical shield to sustain calm under extreme workload pressure.
