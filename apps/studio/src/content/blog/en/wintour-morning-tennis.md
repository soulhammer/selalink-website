Anna Wintour, Dame Commander of the Order of the British Empire (DBE) and Editor-in-Chief of Vogue magazine since 1988, stands as the indisputable authority of the global fashion industry. As Global Chief Content Officer of Condé Nast and the visionary chair of the Met Gala, she is also famously known as the real-life inspiration behind the film 'The Devil Wears Prada'. Where does her razor-sharp judgment and tireless energy behind her signature dark sunglasses come from? 🎾

Even past the age of 70, Anna Wintour wakes up at exactly 5 AM every morning to pick up a tennis racket. Before taking any business call or opening work emails, she rushes onto the court for an hour of intense tennis featuring powerful serves and sprints. Only after drenching herself in morning sweat and boosting blood circulation to peak levels does she put on her sunglasses and enter the office. Her unwavering composure and cold clarity amid daily storms of high-stakes decision-making are direct results of this physical morning discipline.

In this post, we examine the neurophysiological benefits of this decades-long morning tennis routine maintained by fashion's grand icon, and provide a 3-step practical morning reset routine for busy modern professionals.

## 1. Body Temperature Elevation and Stimulation of Neuro-arousal Chemicals

Immediately upon waking, the brain experiences sleep inertia and cognitive sluggishness. Facing intense meetings or stressful emails in this state without first awakening the brain can paralyze prefrontal cortex function, leading to impulsive or emotionally biased decisions.

Engaging in vigorous cardiovascular exercise like tennis in the morning rapidly increases blood flow to brain cells and healthily raises body temperature. This stimulation surges the secretion of <strong>Dopamine</strong>, which accelerates prefrontal function, and norepinephrine, which aids neural alertness. Consequently, it suppresses amygdala hyper-reactivity, building a physical shield that maintains calmness under extreme workload and rapidly shifting trends.

## 3. Don't Forget Time Management and Gentle Heart Warm-Up

Immediately after waking, the heart is constricted and vascular pressure is high. Starting an all-out sprint right out of bed places extreme strain on the cardiovascular system. Perform 3 minutes of gradual warm-up, such as light dynamic stretching and marching in place, to allow the heart to adapt before increasing workout intensity.
