Known as the 'Merry Cocker', the English Cocker Spaniel is a beloved medium-sized gundog treasured worldwide for its exuberant personality and affectionate nature. However, their long, heavy pendulous ears restrict airflow, making them highly susceptible to otitis externa (ear infections). Furthermore, as active flushing dogs, failing to satisfy their high exercise and scentwork needs can lead to stress-induced behavioral issues. Today, PetSelf decodes English Cocker Spaniel body language and outlines a 3-step homecare routine for optimal ear health and vitality.
