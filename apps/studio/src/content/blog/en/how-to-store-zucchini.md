Have you ever bought fresh zucchini only to find it soft, mushy, or moldy after a few days in the fridge? 🥒

Zucchini contains over 90% water, making it extremely sensitive to moisture, temperature fluctuations, and chilling injury. Leaving it unprotected in the refrigerator causes moisture evaporation and skin shriveling, while trapped humidity leads to softening and decay.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on expert guidelines from agricultural and food safety authorities to maximize your zucchini's crispness and shelf life. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never wash zucchini before storing 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Storing wet zucchini traps moisture on the skin, causing it to soften rapidly and creating an ideal environment for mold growth. Wipe off dirt or debris with a dry cloth or paper towel, and wash right before cooking.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Avoid storing at overly cold temperatures ❄️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Zucchini is sensitive to cold temperatures (below 0–2°C) and suffers from chilling injury, leading to pitting, water-soaked spots, and decay. Keep it in the warmer crisper drawer of your refrigerator.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
