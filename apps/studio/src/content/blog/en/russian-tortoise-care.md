faqs:
  - question: "Why is regular lukewarm water 'Soaking' crucial for preventing bladder stones in tortoises?"
    answer: "Russian Tortoises excrete nitrogenous waste as white semi-solid 'uric acid' (urates). When chronically dehydrated, urates precipitate and solidify inside the urinary bladder, forming massive uroliths (bladder stones) that cause fatal blockage. Soaking 2 to 3 times weekly in lukewarm water stimulates drinking, intestinal motility, and safe flushing of urates."
  - question: "Why shouldn't I feed fruits, insects, or commercial dog/cat food to a Russian Tortoise?"
    answer: "Russian Tortoises are 100% strict herbivores adapted to fibrous desert weeds. High-sugar fruits disrupt gut microflora, causing severe protozoal blooms and lethal diarrhea. Animal protein or insect feeding elevates blood uric acid rapidly, inducing visceral gout, kidney failure, and pyramiding shell deformities. Feed exclusively fibrous dark leafy greens and safe weeds."

Native to the arid steppes and deserts of Central Asia, the Russian Tortoise (Agrionemys horsfieldii), also known as Horsfield's Tortoise, is a beloved small diurnal reptile kept worldwide due to its manageable adult size (15–25 cm) and robust hardiness. Possessing four clawed digits on each front paw, Russian Tortoises have an intense instinct for burrowing. However, indoor captive environments expose them to severe Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD) if UVB radiation and dietary calcium are deficient. Furthermore, dehydration causes white uric acid to solidify into lethal bladder stones, while feeding fruit or animal protein triggers severe intestinal microbial collapse and gout. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Russian Tortoise's body language and protect its health.

      <span class="pr-4">Why is regular lukewarm water 'Soaking' crucial for preventing bladder stones in tortoises?</span>

When chronically dehydrated, urates precipitate and solidify inside the urinary bladder, forming massive uroliths (bladder stones) that cause fatal blockage. Soaking 2 to 3 times weekly in lukewarm water stimulates drinking, intestinal motility, and safe flushing of urates.

Animal protein or insect feeding elevates blood uric acid rapidly, inducing visceral gout, kidney failure, and pyramiding shell deformities. Feed exclusively fibrous dark leafy greens and safe weeds.
