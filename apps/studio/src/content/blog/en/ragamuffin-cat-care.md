Derived from the Ragdoll in the 1990s, the Ragamuffin is a large, heavily boned cat breed famous for its dense, plush rabbit-like coat, diverse color patterns, and exceptionally expressive walnut-shaped eyes. Taking 4 to 5 years to reach full physical maturity, Ragamuffins uniquely possess a natural fatty pad on their lower abdomen as outlined in breed standards. Renowned for going completely limp when cradled in human arms, they are ultimate lap cats. However, their plush double coat requires routine grooming, and their slow maturation calls for careful nutrition and monitoring for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM). In this guide, outlines Ragamuffin communication signals and a 3-step daily care guide.
