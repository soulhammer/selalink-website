Have you ever stored pork in the refrigerator only to find a pool of blood (drip), a gamey smell, or a dried-out, discolored surface? 🥩

Pork is rich in moisture, protein, and fat, making it highly susceptible to bacterial growth and lipid oxidation after slaughter. In particular, leaving accumulated purge/drip on the meat's surface creates an ideal environment for microorganisms, leading to foul odors, discoloration, and ruined texture.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step pork storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maximize freshness. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Wipe off purge and coat lightly with cooking oil — Moisture Retention & Microbial Barrier 📦</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never store pork without wiping off excess drip (purge) ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The liquid purge on the surface of pork serves as a prime nutrient source for bacteria and microorganisms to multiply rapidly. Leaving it unchecked intensifies gamey odors and accelerates spoilage, so always pat it dry with paper towels before storage.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Avoid repeated thawing and refreezing 🚫</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Thawing frozen pork breaks down muscle tissue, causing moisture and nutrient loss while inviting rapid bacterial proliferation. Refreezing degrades meat texture to a tough state and poses health risks, so portion pork for single-use meals before freezing and thaw only what you need.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
