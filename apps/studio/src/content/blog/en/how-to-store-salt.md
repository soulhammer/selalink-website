Have you ever opened your salt shaker or jar only to find it damp and clumped together, or noticed the container rusted and ruined? 🧂

Salt is a representative inorganic mineral ingredient without an expiration date because its near-zero water activity prevents bacteria and microbes from growing. However, it readily absorbs moisture under high relative humidity and possesses strong corrosive properties against metals. Improper storage causes clumping, container corrosion, and contamination.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on expert food safety guidelines to keep your salt fine, dry, and usable indefinitely. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Avoid metal containers and refrigeration ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Salt accelerates the rust and corrosion of metal containers like iron or stainless steel, and refrigerating salt causes condensation that turns it damp and runny. Always store salt at room temperature in glass, ceramic, or plastic containers.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Keep away from stove steam and direct heat 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Steam and heat generated while cooking directly reach salt shakers, causing salt particles to draw in moisture and harden. Keep salt shakers in a dry spice cabinet a step away from the cooking area.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
