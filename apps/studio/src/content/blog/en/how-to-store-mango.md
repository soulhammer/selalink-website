Have you ever bought a sweet, creamy mango, put it in the fridge while it was still unripe, and ended up with a blackened skin and tough, flavorless flesh? 🥭

Mangoes are classic climacteric fruits, meaning they continue to ripen and emit ethylene gas even after being harvested. Because they are tropical fruits, exposing them to cold temperatures before they are fully ripe halts the ripening process permanently, causing chilling injury that turns the skin grayish-brown and destroys the flavor forever.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maximize your mango's sweetness and maintain its freshness. 🛡️

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">[Before Ripening] Store at cool room temperature (20–25°C) — Natural Ripening (3–7 Days) 🌡️</h4>

  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">

  </p>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">[After Ripening] Wrap in paper towels and refrigerate in a bag — Delay Over-ripening (5–7 Days) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never put an unripe (firm) mango in the refrigerator ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      If a mango is placed in cold temperatures below 10°C (50°F) before it fully ripens, the ripening process stops permanently. It will suffer chilling injury, resulting in a mottled, blackened skin and rubbery flesh. Always ripen at room temperature first.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">To speed up ripening, store in a paper bag with an ethylene-producing fruit 🍎</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Placing a mango in a loosely closed paper bag alongside a banana or apple (which naturally emit high levels of ethylene gas) will safely accelerate the ripening process by two to three times.
    </p>
