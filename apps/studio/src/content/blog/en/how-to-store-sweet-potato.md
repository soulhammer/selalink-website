Have you ever stored a box of sweet potatoes in the refrigerator or a cold balcony, only to find them discolored, mushy, and rotting? 🍠

Sweet potatoes are tropical/subtropical root crops highly sensitive to cold temperatures and excess moisture. When stored below 10°C—such as in a refrigerator or an unheated winter balcony—they suffer from 'chilling injury,' which breaks down cellular tissue, resulting in decay, internal darkening, and a bitter taste.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step sweet potato storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maximize freshness. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Spread on newspaper immediately after purchase to air-dry — Moisture Removal 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Surface moisture is the leading cause of mold and decay. 💡 Storage Tip: Unpack sweet potatoes and lay them flat on newspaper in a cool, well-ventilated area without overlapping for a day to dry off all surface dampness.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Wrap individually in newspaper and place in a ventilated cardboard box — Ambient Storage (1–2 Months) 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Sweet potatoes continue to respire, emitting moisture and carbon dioxide, making ventilation vital. 💡 Storage Tip: Wrap each dried sweet potato individually in newspaper and layer them into a perforated cardboard box. Newspaper absorbs humidity and prevents bruising.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Store at a cool room temperature of 12–15°C (Freeze cooked only) — Optimal Environment Control ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Raw sweet potatoes store best in a shaded, ventilated room at 12–15°C (e.g., indoors). 💡 Storage Tip: For long-term storage, steam or bake sweet potatoes, let them cool completely, seal in airtight containers, and freeze below -18°C. They remain delicious for up to 6 months.

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Never store raw sweet potatoes in the refrigerator ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Exposing raw sweet potatoes to temperatures below 10°C causes chilling injury that destroys cellular walls. Chilled sweet potatoes develop dark spots, foul odors, and rot rapidly due to fungal growth. Keep raw sweet potatoes at room temperature (12–15°C).
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Open the box immediately upon purchase to dry excess moisture 🚫</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Moisture trapped inside boxes during harvesting or shipping creates an ideal environment for mold. Immediately open the package and spread the sweet potatoes out on newspaper for about 24 hours to air-dry completely before storage.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
