Russian Blues are captivating felines renowned for their short, silvery-blue coat and striking green eyes. While quiet, gentle, and deeply loyal to their family, they are genetically shy, reserved, and highly sensitive to loud noises or environment changes. 🐱 This is why they quickly hide inside cat trees or closets when a guest visits or when a vacuum cleaner runs. Forcing them out of their hiding spots only causes more panic. In this guide, details how to build a safe, stress-free space for your shy Russian Blue and introduces a 3-step routine to build a strong bond with them.
