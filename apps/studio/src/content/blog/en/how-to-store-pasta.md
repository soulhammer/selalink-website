Have you ever loosely tied an opened bag of pasta, stored it in the pantry, and later found it stale, soft, or infested with bugs? 🍝

Dried pasta has a low moisture content (under 12%), making it ideal for long-term storage. However, it readily absorbs ambient moisture, light, and surrounding odors, while easily becoming a target for grain pests like flour moths and weevils. Improper storage degrades the flavor of durum wheat and leads to quality loss.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide recommended by USDA and food safety experts to keep your dried pasta fresh for years. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not store dried pasta in the refrigerator ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Dried pasta easily absorbs ambient moisture. The high humidity inside a refrigerator and condensation from frequent door opening make the pasta soggy and create an ideal environment for mold growth. Always store it at cool room temperature.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not leave opened pasta in its original plastic bag 🐛</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Opened plastic bags have tiny gaps that allow humidity to enter and enable grain pests like meal moth larvae to bore through. Transfer opened pasta immediately to a completely airtight container.
    </p>
  </div>
