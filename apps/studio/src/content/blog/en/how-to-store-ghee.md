Have you ever wondered whether to store Ghee—popularized in keto diets and bulletproof coffee—in the pantry or the refrigerator? 🧈

Ghee is clarified butter produced by slowly heating butter to evaporate moisture and filter out over 99% of milk proteins (casein and whey) and lactose. This clarification process reduces its water activity to $a_w \le 0.2$, creating a biologically stable state where bacteria and mold cannot survive.

In this guide, we share a scientifically proven 3-step Ghee storage guide based on guidelines from FSSAI, USDA, and MFDS to preserve its rich nuttiness and nutritional quality without degradation. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Strictly prevent water drops and food particle contamination 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      While pure Ghee resists microbial growth due to its low water activity ($a_w \le 0.2$), introducing moisture or food crumbs enables microbial proliferation and triggers hydrolytic rancidity. Always use a clean, completely dry spoon when scooping.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Keep away from direct sunlight and heat near cooking stoves ☀️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Although Ghee is high in saturated fats and resistant to rapid oxidation, intense UV rays and heat cause lipid oxidation and destroy its delicate aromatic compounds. Store it in a cool, dark pantry away from heat sources.
    </p>
  </div>
