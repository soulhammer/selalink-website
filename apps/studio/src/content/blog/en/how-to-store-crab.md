Have you ever bought fresh crabs only to find their meat melted away into liquid or smelling fishy after a short time in the fridge? 🦀

When a crab dies, powerful digestive enzymes in its guts trigger a rapid process called autolysis, causing the meat to dissolve quickly. Furthermore, crabs are high-risk seafood prone to rapid bacterial multiplication (such as Vibrio species), making proper storage essential to preserve flesh density and taste.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step crab storage guide based on guidelines from agriculture and food safety authorities to keep your crab meat sweet, firm, and safe. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Wrap live crabs with a damp cloth for short-term refrigeration — Short-term Survival Storage 🦀</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    If you cannot cook live crabs immediately, keep them moist and cold. 💡 Storage Tip: Wrap unwashed live crabs in a damp towel or damp newspaper and place them in a cool container in the refrigerator (1–4°C / 34–39°F). They can stay alive for up to 1 day.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Steaming before storage is safest — Steamed Cold/Freezer Storage (2 Days / 2–3 Months) ♨️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    The best way to preserve crab meat texture and flavor is to steam them right after buying. 💡 Storage Tip: Scrub live crabs clean and steam them upside down (belly facing up). Cool down, double-seal in airtight bags/containers, and refrigerate (2 days) or freeze below -18°C (2–3 months).
  </p>
</div>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Clean and freeze in portions — Raw Freezer Storage (1 Month) 🧊</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never submerge live crabs in fresh tap water for storage ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Submerging live crabs in tap water causes osmotic shock, killing them rapidly and initiating autolysis. Scrub the shells clean with a brush right before cooking.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never leave dead raw crabs uncleaned at room temperature or in the fridge 🦀</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Once dead, gut enzymes penetrate into the muscle tissue, liquefying the meat and generating ammonia and bacteria. Dead raw crabs must be steamed immediately or cleaned and frozen.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
