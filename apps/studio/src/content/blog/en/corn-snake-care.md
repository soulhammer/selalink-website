Named for the corn-like checkered pattern on their belly scales, the Corn Snake (Pantherophis guttatus) is the most cherished starter pet snake worldwide due to its docile nature and striking color morphs. Corn snakes require cooler thermal gradients than Ball Pythons, thriving with a cool zone of 22°C–24°C, a hot zone of 28°C–30°C, and baseline humidity of 40%–50%. Handling within 48 hours post-feeding or inadequate basking temperatures can cause fatal regurgitation. Furthermore, their slender, muscular bodies make them notorious escape artists. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your snake's signals and maintain its health.
