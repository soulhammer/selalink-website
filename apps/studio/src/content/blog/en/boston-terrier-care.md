Known as the 'American Gentleman' for their tuxedo-like coat and sweet demeanor, the Boston Terrier is a cherished companion. However, as a brachycephalic (flat-faced) breed, their shortened airways make them susceptible to Brachycephalic Airway Obstructive Syndrome (BAOS) and heat exhaustion. Additionally, their large, prominent eyes are prone to corneal ulcers and dry eyes. Today, PetSelf decodes the respiratory and ocular distress signals of the Boston Terrier and outlines a 3-step daily care routine to safeguard their health.
