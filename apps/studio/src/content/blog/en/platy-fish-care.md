Instantly recognizable by the black silhouette resembling a Mickey Mouse face at the base of their caudal fin, Mickey Mouse Platies (Xiphophorus maculatus) are among the most popular tropical livebearers worldwide. As livebearers that give birth to free-swimming fry rather than laying eggs, they breed easily; however, sudden temperature drops below 22°C (72°F) or deteriorating water quality make them vulnerable to 'Ich' (White Spot Disease) and fin clamping. Furthermore, adult platies exhibit 'Fry Cannibalism', frequently preying on newborn fry if hiding spaces are absent. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Platy's signals and protect its health.
