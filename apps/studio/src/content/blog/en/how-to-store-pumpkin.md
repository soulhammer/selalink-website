Have you ever bought a firm, sweet pumpkin or squash, only to find mold growing from the stem or the cut surfaces drying out and spoiling in the fridge? 🎃

Pumpkins and hard squashes have thick skins ideal for long-term storage, but they are highly sensitive to temperature and moisture. Whole pumpkins stored in humid or overly cold conditions develop stem rot, while cut pumpkins left with their seeds and pulpy centers deteriorate within days due to bacterial soft rot.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to preserve sweet flavor and dense texture for up to 3 months. 🛡️

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">[Whole] Store upright in a cool, dry place at 10–15°C — Optimal Pantry Storage (1–2 Months) ☀️</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never store whole, uncut pumpkins in the refrigerator ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The optimal storage temperature for whole pumpkins is 10–15°C (50–59°F). Exposing them to cold, humid refrigerator air (0–4°C) causes chilling injury, leading to rapid decay and mold growth around the stem.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Scoop out all seeds and fibrous pulp before storing cut pieces 🥄</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The moist inner cavity containing seeds and pulp is prone to rapid microbial growth. Leaving the seeds attached causes cut pumpkin pieces to turn soft, slimy, and moldy within a few days.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
