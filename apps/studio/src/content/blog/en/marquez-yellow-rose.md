## 1. Why Visual Rituals Offer Creativity and Stability

The ritual of visually placing and observing a specific color or natural object (like a yellow rose) sends a 'safe and emotionally comfortable signal' to the brain. The relaxed brain emits <strong>alpha waves</strong>, which enhance cognitive activity and concentration, accelerating unintended free associations to elevate creative and multi-dimensional ideation.
