Famous for their large, low-set round ears on the sides of their head, the Dumbo Rat (Rattus norvegicus domestica) is a cherished pet rodent loved worldwide for its remarkable dog-like affection and high intelligence. Often referred to as 'pocket puppies,' Dumbo Rats can learn tricks and respond to their names. However, they possess exceptionally delicate respiratory systems highly vulnerable to chronic Mycoplasma pulmonis infections. Environmental hazards such as phenolic oils from softwood (pine/cedar) bedding, excessive dust, and ammonia buildup rapidly damage their lungs. Furthermore, as highly social group animals, housing a rat alone induces severe depression, and females face high risks of mammary tumors. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Dumbo Rat's body language and protect its health.
