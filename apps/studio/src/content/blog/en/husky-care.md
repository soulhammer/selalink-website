Siberian Huskies are famous for their wolf-like looks, enchanting eyes, and history of pulling sleds in Siberian wilderness. Built with unmatched stamina and a pack-oriented mindset, they are active, independent companions. 🐕

However, their polar heritage makes them vulnerable to hot, humid weather, putting them at high risk of heatstroke in summer. Also, because they were bred to run miles daily, failing to satisfy their energy demands leads to stress, which they express through howling or destructive house-chewing.

Today, PetSelf decodes the unique vocalizations of Huskies communicating their boredom and outlines a 3-step home care guide for double coat maintenance and activity management.
