Thomas Watson, the first president of the tech giant IBM. He forced cards and signs with the single word 'THINK' to be placed in every small space across the company. He sat quietly for over 15 minutes every morning without any documents or pens, fully immersed in thought. Explore the efficacy of this simple sign and thinking habit that drove IBM's leap 100 years ago.

## 1. Why Doing Nothing with Detachment Enhances Intellect

When the brain constantly responds to incoming stimuli (emails, notifications), it has no room to organize memories and plan long-term values. When fully detached from inputs and immersed in thought, the brain stabilizes the <strong>salience network</strong>, which screens important information, and organically exchanges information with the default mode network to clear neural traffic and find solutions.
