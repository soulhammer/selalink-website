The African Pygmy Hedgehog is a quiet, fascinating exotic pet known for its twitching snout and cautious nature. Naturally shy, they rely on their quills for defense and paint foamy saliva onto their backs when encountering new scents. Furthermore, if room temperatures drop even slightly, they risk entering fatal hibernation shock (torpor). In this guide, we outline hedgehog quill behaviors, safe self-anointing handling tips, and a 3-step homecare routine to maintain 24-hour warmth and cleanliness.
