The African Pygmy Hedgehog is a quiet, fascinating exotic pet. They are naturally shy, using their quills for defense and painting foamy saliva onto their backs when encountering new scents. Furthermore, they risk fatal hibernation shock if the room temperature drops. Today on PetSelf, we outline hedgehog quill behaviors, self-anointing tips, and a 3-step routine to maintain stable warmth and cleanliness.

## 1. African Pygmy Hedgehog Instincts: Self-Anointing Froth and Quill Defense

When African Pygmy Hedgehogs taste or smell something new, they create foamy saliva and apply it to their quills, a process called self-anointing. This is a normal behavior to camouflage their scent. Conversely, when frightened, they curl into a tight ball and hiss to warn off contact.

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">SIGNAL 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Self-Anointing</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A harmless wild instinct to register and coat their quills with new environmental scents.<br/>
    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> Do not disrupt them or mistake it for a disease. Let them get comfortable with the scent naturally.
  </p>
</div>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Hissing & Curling into a Ball</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A defensive mode indicating severe fear, raising quills to block any physical contact.<br/>
    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> Stop attempting to handle them. Cover them with a soft fleece blanket to create a dark shelter.
  </p>
</div>

## 2. Custom Daily Care Routine

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Morning: Scrubbing Soiled Running Wheels & Dishes</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Since hedgehogs tend to defecate while running on their wheel, clean and sanitize their wheel and dishes every morning.
  </p>
</div>

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Afternoon: Maintaining Room Temperatures Between 24-26°C</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Prevent low body temperature and hibernation by checking heating pads and digital thermostats regularly.
  </p>
</div>

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Evening: Feeding High-Protein Insect Diet & Paw Safety Checks</h4>

  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Provide high-protein insect-based kibble, and check their paws for any cuts caused by running on wheels.
