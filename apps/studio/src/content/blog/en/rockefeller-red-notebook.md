John D. Rockefeller, the legendary entrepreneur who established Standard Oil and accumulated the largest private wealth in history. Where did his rigorous planning and drive come from? 📕

Since childhood, Rockefeller carried a small red pocket ledger (Ledger A) given by his mother. He recorded every single cent spent, charity donations, business meetings, and tasks immediately as they occurred. He believed that great wealth and unwavering focus stem from tracking small resources. This real-time micro-tracking was his secret to keeping his mind clear.

Today, BuildSelf analyzes the cognitive benefits of Rockefeller's 'real-time analog tracking' routine and how to practice it today.

## 1. Real-time Tracking and Prefrontal Executive Control Network Activation

Writing down figures and tasks immediately in a notebook (real-time micro-tracking) strongly stimulates the prefrontal executive control network. This activates self-regulation, which prevents impulsive reactions to external stimuli. In the long term, it strengthens brain plasticity coordinating behavioral control, permanently maximizing attention span and impulse suppression.
