The compact Common Musk Turtle is ideal for beginners due to its resilient nature and minimal tank size requirements. Although they spend most of their time in water, regular dry basking under a UV lamp is essential for shell disinfection, vitamin D synthesis, and maintaining body temperature. Poor water quality and lack of dry basking can lead to painful shell rot (ulcerative shell disease). In this guide, we explain their basking body language and relaxation signals, along with a 3-step tank management routine for crystal-clear water and healthy shell care.
