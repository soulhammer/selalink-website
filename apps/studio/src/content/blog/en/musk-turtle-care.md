The compact Common Musk Turtle is ideal for beginners. Although they spend most of their time in water, regular dry basking under a UV lamp is essential for shell disinfection and vitamin D synthesis. Poor water quality and lack of dry basking can lead to painful shell rot. Today on PetSelf, we explain their basking languages, warning signs, and a 3-step tank management routine for crystal-clear water.
