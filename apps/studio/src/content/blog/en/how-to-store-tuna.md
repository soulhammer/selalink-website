Have you ever put an opened can of tuna straight into the fridge, only to find it smelling metallic, fishy, or turning dark yellow? 🐟

Tuna is rich in protein and healthy omega-3 fatty acids (DHA, EPA), but once exposed to air, lipid oxidation occurs rapidly and fishy odors intensify. In particular, leaving opened canned tuna in its original tin creates a high risk of metal leaching and microbial contamination.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step tuna storage guide recommended by the USDA and food safety authorities to preserve flavor and hygiene. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never store opened canned tuna in its original metal can ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Once opened, contact with air causes the inner tin lining of the can to oxidize, potentially leaching metal into the food and accelerating spoilage. Always transfer leftover canned tuna to a glass or plastic airtight container immediately.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Thoroughly pat dry moisture/drip from fresh tuna 🩸</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Residual drip (blood and moisture) on fresh or raw tuna is a primary cause of bacterial growth and strong fishy odors. Gently wipe away all surface moisture with paper towels before storing.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
