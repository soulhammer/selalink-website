The Jack Russell Terrier is one of the most beloved small working breeds worldwide, made famous through film and media. Originally bred for fox hunting, they possess extraordinary stamina and keen intelligence. Without adequate physical exercise and mental stimulation, they can easily develop stress-induced behavioral issues. Their dense double coat also requires consistent shedding management, while their energetic jumping tendencies necessitate proactive joint and patella care. In this guide, we analyze their instinctive body signals and reveal a 3-step homecare routine for stamina release and overall health.
