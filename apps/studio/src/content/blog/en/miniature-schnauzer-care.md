Famous for their bushy eyebrows and distinctive grandfather beard, the Miniature Schnauzer is a beloved terrier breed known for exceptional intelligence and loyalty. Miniature Schnauzers have a genetic predisposition to idiopathic hyperlipidemia (impaired lipid metabolism), making them the single most vulnerable breed to acute and chronic pancreatitis when fed fatty foods. Additionally, they suffer from a unique genetic follicular condition known as 'Schnauzer Comedone Syndrome' along their back, as well as beard dermatitis and urinary stones (calcium oxalate). In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Schnauzer's body signals and maintain its optimal health.
