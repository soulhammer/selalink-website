Have you ever left a bottle of ketchup on the kitchen counter like a diner, only to notice it darkening into a dull brown with an overly acidic taste? 🥫

Ketchup is naturally resistant to bacterial growth due to the acidity and osmotic pressure from tomatoes, vinegar, sugar, and salt. However, once opened, exposure to oxygen and room heat oxidizes lycopene, dulling its vibrant red color and degrading its sweet, tangy umami flavor.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step ketchup storage guide based on official guidelines from the FDA, USDA, and food safety experts to preserve its rich taste and bright color long after opening. 🛡️

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Pantry before opening, refrigerate immediately after — Refrigerated Storage (180 Days) ❄️</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never freeze ketchup ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Freezing ketchup breaks its emulsion-like structure, causing severe syneresis (liquid-solid separation). When thawed, watery liquid separates from clumpy solids and cannot be remixed, ruining the texture.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Wipe nozzle clean and seal tightly after use 🥫</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Residual ketchup around the cap hardens into a crust and can invite mold growth upon prolonged air exposure. Wipe the nozzle clean with a paper towel immediately after use and seal tightly.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
