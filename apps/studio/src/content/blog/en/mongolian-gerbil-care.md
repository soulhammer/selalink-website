Native to the arid deserts and steppes of Mongolia and Northern China, the Mongolian Gerbil (Meriones unguiculatus) is a cherished pet rodent famed for its furred tail, energetic curiosity, and clean habits. Adapted to desert environments, gerbils produce minimal waste and odor, making them ideal companions. However, they possess a defensive escape mechanism where the skin sheath easily strips off if grasped—a severe trauma known as 'Tail Degloving.' Furthermore, as obligate social animals, housing a gerbil in isolation causes depression and stress seizures. They require deep burrowing bedding (15–20 cm) and desert sand baths instead of water. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Mongolian Gerbil's body language and protect its health.
