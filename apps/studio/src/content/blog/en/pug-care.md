Cherished for centuries for their wrinkled faces, round expressive eyes, and comical affection, the Pug is an iconic companion dog that thrives on human connection. While Pugs are famously gentle and even-tempered, their flat-faced (brachycephalic) anatomy makes them extremely vulnerable to Brachycephalic Obstructive Airway Syndrome (BOAS) and heatstroke. Furthermore, their protruding eyes are prone to corneal ulcers and dry eye syndrome, while their strong appetite makes obesity a major risk factor for breathing difficulties and joint strain. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Pug's body language and protect its health.
