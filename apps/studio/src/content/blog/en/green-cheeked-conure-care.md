Native to the tropical forests of South America, the Green-cheeked Conure (Pyrrhura molinae) is a beloved companion bird cherished for its vibrant plumage, comical personality, and relatively quiet vocal nature compared to other conures. While highly affectionate and fond of snuggling inside hands or clothing, their specialized air sac respiratory system makes them extremely susceptible to airborne toxins like PTFE (Teflon) cookware fumes, aerosol sprays, and scented oils. Additionally, high-fat seed diets cause fatty liver disease and Vitamin A deficiency, while overstimulating hormonal behavior can trigger fatal Egg Binding in females. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Green-cheeked Conure's body language and protect its health.
