## 2. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Do not wash before storage — Protect the bloom 🫐</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    The white powder on blueberry skins is a natural wax layer called 'Bloom' that blocks mold and moisture loss. Washing them early strips this layer, causing berries to absorb water, soften, and mold quickly.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Line container with paper towels and spread flat — Moisture control 📰</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Line the bottom of a wide container with paper towels and place the berries in a thin layer. The towel absorbs condensation, preventing berries from getting crushed or rotting from sitting in water.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Store unsealed in the crisper drawer — Refrigeration (10 Days) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Keep them in a ventilated container or leave the lid slightly ajar. Put them in the crisper drawer (0-2°C). Blueberries are cold-hardy; keeping them in the coldest back section prevents condensation.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Do not wash before storage 🫐</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Washing removes the natural protective coating (bloom) and introduces moisture, leading to quick spoilage.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Sort out damaged berries ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Mold spreads rapidly between berries. A single moldy blueberry can ruin the entire container.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
