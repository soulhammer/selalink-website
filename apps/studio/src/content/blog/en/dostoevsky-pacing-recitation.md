## 1. Science of How Physical Acceleration and Oral Reading Wake Brain Language Nets

Physical movement of pacing the room stimulates the cerebral motor cortex, immediately accelerating cerebral blood flow and oxygen supply, while oral cognition of speaking dialogues aloud integrates and activates language control areas like Broca's and Wernicke's. Also, acting as a character directly runs the brain's <strong>mirror neurons</strong>, breathing first-person vividness into prose instead of abstract third-person text.
