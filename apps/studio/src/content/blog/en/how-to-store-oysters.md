Have you ever bought fresh raw oysters only to find them smelling off or spoiled after just a day in the fridge? 🦪

Oysters are highly perishable shellfish rich in moisture and active enzymes, making them particularly vulnerable to bacterial contamination (e.g., Vibrio species and Norovirus). Soaking them in tap water or sealing live oysters in airtight containers accelerates decay, making proper cold storage essential.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on safety guidelines from the FDA, USDA, and MFDS to keep your oysters safe and fresh. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never submerge live oysters in fresh tap water ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Live oysters in the shell are sensitive to osmotic shock. Submerging them in tap water kills them quickly and triggers rapid decomposition. Do not wash live oysters until immediately before consumption.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never store live oysters in an airtight container 🌬️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Unshucked oysters are living organisms. Sealing them tightly suffocates them. Keep them in an open container covered with a damp towel so they can breathe while retaining humidity.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
