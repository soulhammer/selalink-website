The Sphynx cat is a unique and captivating breed born without a typical coat due to a natural genetic mutation. Because they lack fur to absorb skin oils, sebum produced by their sebaceous glands accumulates directly on the skin surface, leading to brownish residue, clogged pores, and dermatitis if not cleaned regularly. 🐈 Furthermore, without a coat to insulate them, they are highly sensitive to colder temperatures and lose body heat rapidly. Today, PetSelf outlines a tailored 3-step home care routine to hygienically manage your Sphynx's skin sebum while keeping them warm and cozy.
