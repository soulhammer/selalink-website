Rainer Maria Rilke, the unmatched Austrian poet who marked the peak of modern poetry by describing deep inner landscapes in gorgeous rhythms in 'The Notebooks of Malte Laurids Brigge' and 'Duino Elegies'. What unique writing environment did he choose to awaken creative tension and intuitive inspiration? ✍️

Rilke felt that slumping in a chair made his brain lazy and dulled his sharp artistic inspiration. Accordingly, he placed a high-legged standing desk (Stehpult) in the middle of his room. He wrote poems, novels, and countless letters standing all day with his spine straight. The standing posture, supporting his body against gravity, kept Rilke's brain cells in a state of alert tension, serving as the source of deep poetic arousal. For Rilke, the standing desk was a physical trigger that synchronized body and mind into a creative flow.

## 1. 'Standing Effect' to Boost Cerebral Blood Flow and Cognitive Alertness

Slumping in a chair restricts lung expansion and shallows breathing, reducing oxygen flow to the brain. In contrast, standing at a desk contracts large muscles (legs, pelvis, back) against gravity, slightly raising the heart rate. Neuroscientifically, this increases cerebral blood flow by 10-15%, boosting oxygen and glucose supply. The subtle physical tension stimulates the reticular activating system, promoting norepinephrine and dopamine release, which dramatically wakes up the brain's attentional control speed and working memory.
