Boasting magnificent gown-like fins and regal swimming elegance, the Betta fish is a jewel-like companion among aquatic pets. According to WSAVA aquatic guidelines, Bettas possess a specialized 'labyrinth organ' allowing them to breathe atmospheric oxygen directly at the surface, making resting aquatic plants or leaf beds near the surface essential. Maintaining low water currents and stable water temperatures (24°C ~ 26°C) is key to preventing debilitating Fin Rot.

## 2. Betta Fish (Betta splendens / Siamese Fighting Fish)'s Body Language & Signals

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">Body Signal 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Gill Covers Expanded & Fins Fully Fan-Out (Flaring)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A territorial alert against intrusion or an aggressive stress response triggered by seeing their own mirror reflection.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Remove mirrors or reflective objects beside the tank to ensure flaring episodes do not exceed 5 minutes.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">Body Signal 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Lying Still on Leaves Near Water Surface (Labyrinth Resting)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> Normal resting behavior easing fin weight while breathing atmospheric air via the labyrinth organ during deep sleep.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> This is not an illness; dim the aquarium lights and adjust the sponge filter to ensure the current is not too strong.
  </p>
