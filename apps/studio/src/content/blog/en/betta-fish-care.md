Boasting magnificent gown-like fins and regal swimming elegance, the Betta fish is a jewel-like companion among aquatic pets. According to WSAVA aquatic guidelines, Bettas possess a specialized 'labyrinth organ' allowing them to breathe atmospheric oxygen directly at the surface, making resting aquatic plants or leaf beds near the surface essential. Maintaining low water currents and stable water temperatures (24°C ~ 26°C) is key to preventing debilitating Fin Rot.
