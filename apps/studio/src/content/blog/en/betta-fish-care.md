Boasting magnificent flowing fins and regal elegance, Betta fish are jewel-like aquatic companions. According to WSAVA aquatic guidelines, Bettas possess a specialized 'labyrinth organ' allowing them to breathe atmospheric oxygen directly at the surface. Providing low water current, warm stable temperatures (24°C ~ 26°C), and resting leaf hammocks near the surface is essential to prevent debilitating Fin Rot.
