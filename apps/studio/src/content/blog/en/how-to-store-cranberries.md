When buying fresh cranberries for salads or juices, have you ever worried they might spoil or grow mold quickly like strawberries or blueberries? 🫐

Cranberries naturally contain rich amounts of benzoic acid—a potent natural preservative—and organic acids, creating a strongly acidic environment with a pH between 2.3 and 2.5. This endows them with superior natural antibacterial and antifungal properties compared to most berries. However, when moisture evaporates, micro-cracks form in the skin, allowing mold to invade. Thus, maintaining 90–95% relative humidity while regulating respiration is the scientific secret to long storage.

In this guide, we share a scientifically proven 3-step cranberry storage guide based on guidelines from the USDA, FDA, and MFDS to safely preserve their tart flavor and firm texture. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Prevent skin micro-cracks and mold invasion caused by moisture loss ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      When cranberries lose moisture, micro-cracks develop in their skin, rendering them vulnerable to mold infection. Storing them in perforated plastic bags maintains high relative humidity (90–95%) to prevent transpiration.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">

  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not wash cranberries prior to storage 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Washing raw cranberries before storage strips away their protective natural wax layer and leaves moisture trapped on the skin, inviting mold growth. Always wash them right before consumption.
    </p>
