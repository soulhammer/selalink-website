German Shepherds are renowned worldwide as top police, military, and family dogs due to their exceptional intelligence, bravery, and loyalty. However, without adequate physical and mental stimulation matching their strong working drive, they can develop boundary aggression or destructive behaviors from stress. Additionally, their body structure makes them prone to hip and elbow dysplasia, and their dense double coat results in heavy shedding. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your German Shepherd's body language and keep their joints and coat healthy.
