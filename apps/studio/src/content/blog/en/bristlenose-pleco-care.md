The Bristlenose Pleco (*Ancistrus* spp.) is a world-renowned freshwater catfish treasured by aquarists for its unmatched algae-eating efficiency and charming bushy snout. Mature males develop prominent tentacles (bristles) across their snout, setting them apart from other armored catfishes. Growing to a manageable 10–15 cm (4–6 inches), they suit small-to-medium community aquariums far better than giant common plecos. To maintain optimal gut health, providing natural driftwood is crucial, as they ingest wood fibers (lignin) required for proper digestion. Because aquarium algae alone cannot sustain them, supplementary blanched vegetables (zucchini, pumpkin, spinach) are vital. In this guide, breaks down Bristlenose Pleco behaviors and offers a 3-step daily care routine.
