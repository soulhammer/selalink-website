The Eclectus Parrot (*Eclectus roratus*) is a world-renowned medium-large parrot species famed for its extreme sexual dimorphism. Males boast emerald green plumage with a candy-corn orange beak, while females showcase a stunning crimson and violet-blue coat with a black beak—a contrast so stark they were historically mistaken for two distinct species. Anatomically, Eclectus Parrots possess significantly longer digestive tracts (proventriculus and intestines) than other parrots, requiring a high-fiber, high-moisture diet rich in fresh fruits, vegetables, and complex carbohydrates. Feeding synthetic-fortified pellets or high-fat seed diets can trigger neurological distress such as toe-tapping and wing-flipping. In this guide, decodes Eclectus Parrot body signals and shares a 3-step nutrition and care guide.
