## 1. Why Reading Narrative Text Improves Brain Judgment

Unlike viewing fragmented words (bullet points), reading a complete narrative report intensively activates the brain's <strong>prefrontal cortex</strong>. The prefrontal cortex is the hub for in-depth analysis of causal relationships and making logical decisions. Reading prose increases information processing density in this region, preventing misjudgments.
