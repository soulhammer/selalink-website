Have you ever stored fresh grapes in the refrigerator only to find them dropping off the stem, turning mushy, or covered in gray mold? 🍇

Grapes have very thin skins and are densely packed along the cluster stem. Excessive humidity causes gray mold fungus (Botrytis) to thrive, while dry conditions wither the stems, causing berries to drop rapidly. Protecting their natural wax layer (bloom) and controlling moisture are the keys to freshness.

In this guide, shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on guidelines from the USDA and RDA to preserve the natural bloom, prevent berry drop, and keep your grapes firm and sweet for over 2 weeks. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Never wash with water before storage — Preserve Natural Bloom 🍇</h4>

  </p>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not stack heavy items on top or crush bunches ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Grapes are easily crushed under light pressure. Leaking juice from burst grapes accelerates rot across the entire cluster. Store bunches in a single layer without anything placed on top.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Remove damaged or squishy grapes before storage 🍇</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      If bruised or burst grapes remain within the cluster, the leaking juice will quickly spoil neighboring healthy berries. Carefully trim away damaged grapes with scissors before storing.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
