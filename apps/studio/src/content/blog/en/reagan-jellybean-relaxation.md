## 1. Mastication and Sensory Substitution Stabilizing the Amygdala

The rhythmic movement of chewing activates trigeminal nerves, reducing the activity of the amygdala which controls fear and stress, lowering cortisol.

At the same time, when harmful cravings strike, inserting a safe substitute sensor (like chewing jellybeans) acts as Oral Sensory Substitution, satisfying dopamine reward circuits without damage.
