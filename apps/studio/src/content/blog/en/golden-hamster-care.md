The small and adorable Golden Hamster is a beloved solitary pet. However, they retain their wild instincts of digging burrows and running miles daily. In confined cages or noisy surroundings, they easily suffer from extreme stress. Stressed hamsters may grind their teeth loudly or chew cage bars, risking broken teeth. Today on PetSelf, we explain the warning signals of Golden Hamsters and introduce a 3-step daily care routine for a cozy, stress-free day.
