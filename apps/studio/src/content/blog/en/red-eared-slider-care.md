Famous for the distinctive red stripe behind their eyes, the Red-Eared Slider (Trachemys scripta elegans) is the most widely recognized semi-aquatic turtle in the world. They require both a deep aquatic environment for swimming and a dry basking area equipped with heat and light to regulate body temperature. Without proper UVB lighting and heat lamps, sliders cannot synthesize Vitamin D3, leading to Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD) and Shell Rot. Furthermore, cold water temperatures dramatically increase the risk of fatal pneumonia. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your turtle's body signals and maintain shell and respiratory health.
