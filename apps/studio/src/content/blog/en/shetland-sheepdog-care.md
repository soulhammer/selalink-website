Renowned for their lush mane and elegant coat, the Shetland Sheepdog (or Sheltie) is a working herding breed boasting incredible intelligence and devotion. However, their acute senses and high noise sensitivity often trigger reactive barking and alarm behaviors. Furthermore, Shelties carry a high genetic risk for the MDR1 gene mutation, which allows certain antiparasitic medications to cross the blood-brain barrier and cause severe neurotoxicity. In addition, neglecting their dense double coat can cause severe skin irritation and matted fur. Today, PetSelf decodes Sheltie distress signals and outlines a 3-step daily care routine.
