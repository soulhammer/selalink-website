Made world-famous as the main character in 'Finding Nemo', the Clownfish is a standout marine species recognized by its vibrant orange body and three distinct white bands. Clownfish share a classic mutualistic relationship with sea anemones, swimming safely among stinging tentacles thanks to a specialized mucus coating while providing the anemone with food remnants and aeration. Functionally, they are sequential hermaphrodites; if the dominant female dies, the largest male transitions into a female to maintain the social structure. In this guide, breaks down clownfish behavioral signals and shares a 3-step care routine to regulate salinity (specific gravity 1.020–1.025) and nitrogen parameters.
