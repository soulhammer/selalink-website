Native to South America, the Quaker Parrot (Monk Parakeet) is a cherished medium-small parrot renowned worldwide for its exceptional mimicry skills, bold charm, and affectionate nature. Unique among parrots, Quakers build massive communal stick nests in the wild, instilling in them a fiercely protective instinct over their home cage. However, their voracious appetite makes them highly prone to obesity and fatal Hepatic Lipidosis (Fatty Liver Disease) if fed a high-fat seed diet. Furthermore, their specialized air sac respiratory system renders them extremely vulnerable to airborne toxins such as PTFE (Teflon) cookware fumes. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Quaker's body language and protect its health.
