Pomeranians are highly popular toy dogs, beloved for their fluffy double coat and confident, active gait. They are outgoing and intelligent, but their Spitz heritage can make them sensitive to sounds and prone to sharp barking. 🐕 Pomeranian owners often face two significant health concerns: Patellar Luxation (dislocated kneecaps due to thin bone structure) and Tracheal Collapse (which causes a honking, goose-like cough). These conditions can easily worsen in indoor environments with slippery floors. In this guide, provides key insights into Pomeranian body language and introduces a protective 3-step home care routine for their joints and trachea.
