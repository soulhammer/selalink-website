Have you ever bought fresh, coral-pink salmon only to find its color fading, smell turning fishy, or texture going soft the next day? 🍣

Salmon is high in moisture and rich in healthy omega-3 unsaturated fatty acids, making it exceptionally vulnerable to rapid lipid oxidation (rancidity) and bacterial spoilage upon exposure to oxygen. In particular, the liquid exudate (drip) leaking from salmon skin and flesh serves as the primary source of fishy odors (trimethylamine) and microbial growth.

Today, FreshSnap shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on hygiene guidelines from the FDA and USDA to eliminate fishy odors, prevent oxidation and freezer burn, and keep your salmon fresh. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Check color changes and flesh elasticity carefully ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      If raw salmon loses its vibrant coral-pink or orange hue and turns dull grayish-brown, or if a finger press leaves a permanent indentation without springing back, the protein structure has broken down. Discard it immediately if it smells sour or feels slimy.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Remove skin and pin bones before freezing ❄️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      For long-term freezing (60–90 days), the fat beneath the skin can rancidify first. Trimming pin bones and skin, portioning into single servings, brushing lightly with oil, and vacuum-wrapping tightly preserves optimal texture upon thawing.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
