The Black-tailed Prairie Dog is a highly social rodent native to North American plains. Because their incisors grow continuously, providing unlimited high-fiber Timothy hay is critical to prevent fatal malocclusion and odontoma. Deep burrowing substrate and intense social interaction are essential. Today, PetSelf outlines a 3-step care routine.
