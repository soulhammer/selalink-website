Renowned for their smiling faces and feathery external gills, Axolotls are unique aquatic amphibians capable of incredible limb regeneration. According to Amphibian Ark veterinary standards, Axolotls breathe through cutaneous skin absorption and external gills, making them extremely vulnerable to temperatures above 20°C. Maintaining cool water (16°C ~ 18°C) and bare-bottom tank setups is mandatory.
