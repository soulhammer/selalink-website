Beloved for their smiling expressions and pink external gills, the Axolotl is a rare amphibian pet with extraordinary body regeneration capabilities. According to the International Amphibian Ark Veterinary Protocol, axolotls breathe through cutaneous skin absorption and external gills, making them extremely vulnerable to water temperatures above 20°C. Keeping the water temperature cool at 16°C ~ 18°C and avoiding gravel substrates that can be swallowed are the core elements of their health care.
