Renowned for their muscular physique, high intelligence, and unyielding loyalty, the Rottweiler is a cherished guardian and family companion worldwide. While deeply affectionate with their families, their deep-chested build makes them extremely susceptible to life-threatening Gastric Dilatation-Volvulus (GDV/Bloat) if exercised right after meals. Additionally, their heavy frame places them at high risk for Hip Dysplasia (HD) and ligament injuries, requiring strict weight management, early socialization, and structured obedience training. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily routine to decode your Rottweiler's body language and safeguard their health.
