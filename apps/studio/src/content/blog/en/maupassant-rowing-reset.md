## 1. BDNF Secretion & Brain Blood Flow Redistribution via Cardio

Prolonged brain activity overheats the prefrontal cortex.

Full-body intense cardio like rowing boosts <strong>BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)</strong> and redistributes brain blood flow to muscles as a physical nerve shower.
