Boasting a lush snow-white double coat and a keen, watchful nature, the Japanese Spitz is a highly intelligent and devoted companion dog. However, as a small-to-medium breed, they are genetically predisposed to Patellar Luxation (dislocation of the kneecap). Additionally, their pure white fur makes tear staining around the eyes noticeable, and their dense undercoat requires structured grooming, especially during seasonal shedding. Today, PetSelf decodes the joint pain and behavioral signals of the Japanese Spitz and outlines a 3-step daily care routine for joint protection and coat maintenance.
