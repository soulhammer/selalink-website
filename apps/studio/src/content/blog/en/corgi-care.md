Welsh Corgis are loved for their short legs and fluffy backsides. As former herding dogs, they possess high intelligence, great active energy, and bold personalities. However, their long backs and short legs place heavy structural stress on their spine and joints. Obesity further increases the risk of spinal disc disease, making thorough daily management essential. Today, PetSelf explores Corgi body language and introduces a 3-step homecare routine to protect their spine and joints safely for life.
