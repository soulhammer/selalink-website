The German Blue Ram (Mikrogeophagus ramirezi) is a South American dwarf cichlid celebrated worldwide for its vibrant, jewel-like coloration and graceful swimming among aquascapes. Reaching just 5 to 7 cm in length, they possess a peaceful nature compared to larger cichlids, making them popular tank centerpieces. However, they are sensitive to nitrates and poor water quality, requiring warm temperatures (26–30°C) and clean, soft, slightly acidic water (pH 6.0–7.0). During breeding, they display distinct territorial behaviors, making plants and driftwood structures essential to break line-of-sight. In this guide, we decode their instinctive signals and share a 3-step homecare routine for water stability, territory peace, and pairing care.
