Shiba Inus are beloved globally for their fox-like appearance and curled tail. However, they possess a cat-like independence, strong will, and alert nature. 🐕 Shibas are notoriously sensitive to having their paws, ears, or tail touched. If forced during grooming or nail clipping, they often emit a high-pitched Shiba Scream in protest. Moreover, they have a thick double coat that sheds heavily during seasonal changes. In this guide, we explore how to respect your Shiba's boundaries while maintaining their coat with a 3-step daily care routine.
