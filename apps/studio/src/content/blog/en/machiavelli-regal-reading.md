## 1. Enclothed Cognition Effect and Resetting the Brain's Attention Control State

The human cognitive system is strongly attuned to the symbolic meaning of the clothing worn. While casual homewear sends relaxation signals to the brain, lowering the focus threshold, formal and neat attire instantly drives the prefrontal target control mechanisms that regulate attention. The physical fit of the clothes and neat tactile stimuli deactivate the default mode network of the cerebral cortex, instead activating the executive control network dedicated to precise information processing.
