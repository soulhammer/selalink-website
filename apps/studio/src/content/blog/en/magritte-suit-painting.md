"I don't need to fill my daily life with madness for art. When my daily life is extremely simple and neat, the surrealism on my canvas gains sharp persuasiveness." 🎩

René Magritte (1898–1967) was Belgium's master surrealist painter who overturned conventional thinking by writing 'This is not a pipe' beneath a painting of a pipe. In contrast to his bizarre, reality-deconstructing artistic visions, his daily routine was notoriously neat and disciplined.

Magritte differed completely from bohemian artists who pulled all-nighters covered in paint. Every morning, dressed in a dark suit and bowler hat like a gentleman commuting to an office, he stood before an easel in a corner of his Brussels dining room. He completed his paintings with mathematical precision, never spilling a drop of paint on his clothes or rug. From a brain science perspective, this dress ritual stimulates the prefrontal cortex's cognitive control system, intuitively switching the brain into work mode.

Modern remote workers frequently experience blurred boundaries between work and life. Sitting at a desk in comfortable pajamas causes cognitive confusion as the brain wanders between relaxation and arousal due to the lack of a physical gear-shift device.

This post analyzes how Magritte's suit painting routine controls brain waves and psychology through clothing, presenting a 3-step guide for knowledge workers to instantly switch into Deep Work mode.

## 1. Enclothed Cognition Effect and Brain Science of Prefrontal Cortex Cognitive Control

Human brains experience <strong>Enclothed Cognition</strong>, where clothing's symbolic meaning and physical sensation directly influence cognitive processes.

When wearing neat formal attire or work clothes, the brain subconsciously primes social identities such as professionalism, diligence, and responsibility.

This psychological setup stimulates the cognitive control system of the <strong>Prefrontal Cortex</strong>, filtering distractions and immediately activating work immersion mode even in a comfortable living room.

## 3. Rule for Avoiding Overly Constricting Suits and Finding the Right Neat Workwear Balance

Avoid wearing clothes that overly constrict the body or cause pain just to achieve Enclothed Cognition. Physical discomfort sends fatigue signals to the <strong>Prefrontal Cortex</strong>, impairing concentration. The essence is to maintain neatness while establishing a comfortable balance (such as neat slacks or collared shirts) suitable for prolonged immersion.
