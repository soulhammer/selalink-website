What is the secret of the master picking up trash himself?

## 1. Creative Refresh to the Brain by Altruistic Walking

The act of walking lightly while organizing the surrounding environment gently activates the brain's <strong>default mode network</strong>. This network runs when relaxed, away from explicit cognitive tasks, and helps combine scattered ideas into novel insights. Physical cleanup also stimulates morning serotonin to refresh your mood.
