Named for the distinct cranial crest bumps and marbled patterns reminiscent of stone gargoyles, the Gargoyle Gecko is a captivating arboreal lizard native to southern New Caledonia. According to International Herpetological Society (IHS) care protocols, Gargoyle Geckos are extremely sensitive to temperatures above 28°C and require cool living conditions alongside a structured humidity gradient (50%-60% during the day, 70%-80% at night). Unlike Crested Geckos, Gargoyle Geckos can fully regenerate their tails, making diet (Crested Gecko Diet/CGD) and environmental control the key factors for their longevity.
