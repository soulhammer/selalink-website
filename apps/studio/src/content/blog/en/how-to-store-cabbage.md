Have you ever bought a whole head of cabbage and put it in the fridge, only to find the cut surface turning black and withered, forcing you to throw half of it away? 🥬

Even after being harvested, cabbage continuously draws water and nutrients from its outer leaves through its thick central core to keep growing. If the core is left intact, moisture evaporates rapidly from the inside out, making the leaves limp and accelerating rot.

Today, FreshSnap shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on research from the RDA and USDA to unlock the secret of the cabbage core, keeping your cabbage crisp and fresh for up to 30 days. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Replace the wet paper towel regularly ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Leaving the damp paper towel stuffed inside the core cavity for over a week can trap moisture, causing bacteria or mold to breed and creating a sour odor. For optimal hygiene, replace it with a fresh, damp paper towel every 5 to 7 days.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Never store cabbage at warm room temperature 🥬</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Leaving cabbage at warm room temperatures (above 15°C / 59°F) increases its respiration rate, causing the outer leaves to loosen and moisture to evaporate rapidly. Always store cabbage in the cold crisper drawer (0–4°C) to maintain crispness.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
