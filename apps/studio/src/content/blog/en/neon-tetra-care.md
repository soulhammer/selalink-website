Renowned for its striking blue neon stripe and vibrant red tail, the Neon Tetra (Paracheirodon innesi) is an iconic freshwater characin in the aquarium hobby. Possessing a strong natural schooling behavior, they must be kept in groups of at least 6 to 10 individuals to prevent severe stress. Neon Tetras are sensitive to sudden water parameter changes (pH shock, ammonia spikes) and are vulnerable to 'Neon Tetra Disease'—an incurable microsporidian parasitic infection. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Neon Tetra's color and behavioral signals and maintain a pristine aquarium environment.
