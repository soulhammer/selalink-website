Scottish Folds capture hearts worldwide with their forward-folded ears, round eyes, and owl-like or bear-like appearance. They possess a quiet, sweet, and gentle nature, enjoying peaceful companionship right by their owner's side. 🐈

However, behind the charm of their folded ears lies a serious genetic condition called Osteochondrodysplasia (hereditary cartilage disease). The same gene responsible for folding their ear cartilage also affects bone and cartilage development throughout their body, leading to joint stiffness and chronic pain in their limbs and tail. Furthermore, because their folded ears restrict natural airflow, moisture and debris build up easily, making them highly susceptible to chronic ear mites and otitis externa (ear infections).

Today, PetSelf decodes the physical signals Scottish Folds display when experiencing pain and outlines a tailored 3-step care routine to minimize joint impact and maintain optimal ear hygiene.
