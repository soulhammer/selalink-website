"I have bathed my feet in cold water every morning, for 60 years, and have fallen into no other habit which could be called singular..." 🧊

Thomas Jefferson (1743–1826) was the primary author of the American Declaration of Independence (1776) and the 3rd President of the United States. Living to the remarkable age of 83 while maintaining an extraordinary output as a statesman, philosopher, architect, and polymath, Jefferson attributed his lifelong vigor to an unwavering daily morning ritual.

Every single morning for six decades, immediately upon stepping out of bed at dawn, Jefferson immersed his feet into a basin filled with cold water. As explicitly documented in his famous March 21, 1819 letter to Dr. Vine Utley, Jefferson maintained this cold foot bath habit religiously for 60 years. He asserted that this physical cold stimulus instantly dispelled morning grogginess and sleep inertia, strengthening his immune system and keeping him free from illness throughout his long career.

In today's post, we examine the cognitive neuroscience behind Thomas Jefferson's 'cold foot bath awakening' routine and present a modern 3-step protocol to activate noradrenaline and clear morning brain fog safely.

## 1. Autonomic Nervous System Stimulation and Noradrenaline Brain Arousal

Local cold exposure to cutaneous peripheral nerves in the feet stimulates sympathetic circuits within the <strong>Autonomic Nervous System</strong>, triggering the locus coeruleus to immediately secrete <strong>Noradrenaline (Norepinephrine)</strong>. This key neurotransmitter surges cortical arousal, rapidly clearing morning <strong>Brain Fog</strong> (sleep inertia) and restoring executive focus in the <strong>Prefrontal Cortex</strong>.

## 3. Caution: Protocol for Preventing Blood Pressure Spikes and Ensuring Safe Arousal

Sudden extreme cold stress can strain the cardiovascular system or cause transient blood pressure spikes. If you have hypertension or cardiovascular conditions, begin with cool rather than icy water, and strictly limit foot immersion to 2 or 3 minutes to deliver a gentle, safe autonomic awakening signal.
