Revered as the 'King of Goldfish' for their rounded egg-shaped body, lack of a dorsal fin, and prominent headgrowth (Wen), Ranchu Goldfish (Carassius auratus) are iconic ornamental fish beloved worldwide. However, their highly compressed body structure leaves their internal organs compacted, making them extremely susceptible to 'Swim Bladder Disorder' if they gulp air while eating floating pellets or suffer from constipation. Furthermore, missing a dorsal fin makes them weak swimmers easily exhausted by strong filter currents, while their high waste output requires robust filtration to prevent ammonia spikes and bacterial infections in their delicate headgrowth. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Ranchu's signals and protect its health.
