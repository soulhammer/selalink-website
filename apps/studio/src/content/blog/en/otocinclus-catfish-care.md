The Otocinclus Catfish (Otocinclus spp.) is a tiny suckermouth armored catfish native to the Amazon River basin, widely celebrated as the ultimate 'natural algae cleaner' for planted aquariums globally. Reaching a compact adult size of just 3–4 cm (1.2–1.5 inches), they are exceptionally peaceful and will never attack or harass other fish, dwarf shrimp, or invertebrates. However, because their wild diet consists almost entirely of micro-algae and soft bio-films coating plant leaves and decor, introducing them to newly set up or overly clean tanks frequently results in fatal starvation. Furthermore, they are highly sensitive to fluctuating water parameters and unestablished nitrogen cycles, requiring introduction only into fully matured aquariums via gradual acclimation. Today on PetSelf, we detail Otocinclus behavior signals, nutritional supplementation, and a 3-step care routine.
