Basil pesto brings a rich, fragrant flavor of fresh basil, pine nuts, garlic, and parmesan cheese to pasta and sandwiches! 🌿🧀

The vibrant green color and fresh aroma are the heart of basil pesto. However, once exposed to air, the chlorophyll in basil oxidizes rapidly into a dull brown color, and the oil-submerged herbs and garlic create an anaerobic environment where Clostridium botulinum bacteria can thrive if improperly stored.

In this guide, based on scientifically verified guidelines from the USDA, FDA, and MFDS, we present a 3-step optimal storage strategy to prevent chlorophyll breakdown (browning) and eliminate botulism risks. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Pour a layer of extra virgin olive oil over the surface — Oil Film Oxygen Barrier 🫒</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Transfer pesto into a container, smooth the top surface flat with a spoon, and pour a 2–3 mm layer of extra virgin olive oil over it. This oil film completely blocks oxygen contact, preventing chlorophyll from oxidizing into brown pheophytin.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Refrigerate strictly at or below 0–4°C — Botulism Prevention Cold Storage (7–14 Days) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Store the oil-sealed pesto in an airtight container deep inside the refrigerator at 0–4°C (32–39°F). Low temperature is mandatory to inhibit Clostridium botulinum toxin production. Consume opened store-bought or homemade pesto within 7 to 14 days.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Portion into ice cube trays and freeze for long-term storage — Freezer Storage (3–6 Months) 🧊</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    If you have excess pesto, spoon single-use portions into ice cube trays and freeze. Once solid, transfer the pesto cubes into a Ziploc freezer bag at -18°C (0°F) or below. You can pop out single servings whenever needed while keeping color and aroma for 3 to 6 months.
  </p>
</div>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never leave opened or homemade basil pesto at room temperature ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Submerging fresh herbs, garlic, and nuts in olive oil creates an anaerobic (oxygen-free) environment that encourages the growth of Clostridium botulinum, a bacterium producing deadly neurotoxins. Always refrigerate immediately at or below 4°C.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not leave exposed to air and light 🌿</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The green pigment in basil, chlorophyll, reacts with oxygen and light to convert into brown pheophytin, causing rapid browning and loss of fresh flavor. Always seal tightly after use.
    </p>
  </div>
