Boasting incredibly soft fur and adorable round bodies, Chinchillas are popular exotic pets. However, unlike cats and dogs, their teeth grow continuously throughout their lives, making them highly prone to painful malocclusion. They also lack sweat glands and have extremely dense fur, making them highly vulnerable to heatstroke and moisture. Today, PetSelf decodes Chinchilla dental and heat signals, and presents a 3-step care routine.
