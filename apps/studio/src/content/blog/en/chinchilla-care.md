Boasting silky-soft fur and adorable round bodies, Chinchillas are popular exotic pets. However, unlike dogs and cats, a chinchilla's teeth grow continuously throughout its life. As a result, misaligned incisors and molars often pierce the mouth, leading to painful malocclusion. Furthermore, lacking sweat glands and possessing extremely dense fur, they are highly vulnerable to heatstroke in hot, humid environments. Today, PetSelf decodes chinchilla dental pain and heat stress signals, proposing a 3-step care routine to safeguard their health.
