The Crystal Red Shrimp (Caridina logemanni var. 'Crystal Red', commonly known as CRS) is a premium freshwater dwarf crustacean celebrated globally for its striking red and white banded patterns. Continuously grazing on micro-biofilm across aquarium plants and substrate, CRS offer captivating visual appeal. However, they are exceptionally sensitive to minute fluctuations in water parameters and temperature. In particular, improper levels of nitrogenous wastes (ammonia, nitrite), Total Dissolved Solids (TDS), or General Hardness (GH) during the molting process often result in fatal dysecdysis (molting failure). Maintaining an active soil substrate that buffers water to a slightly acidic pH, pairing it with sponge filtration, and using Remineralized Reverse Osmosis (RO) water are vital to their survival. Today on PetSelf, we explain CRS behavioral signals, precise water parameter controls, and a 3-step daily care routine.
