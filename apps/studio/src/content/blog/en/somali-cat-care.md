Renowned for their plume tail and elegant ticked coat, the Somali Cat is a striking, energetic breed originating from the longhair gene of the Abyssinian. Brimming with curiosity and intelligence, they are often dubbed the 'clowns of the cat kingdom' for their agile athleticism and inquisitive nature. However, behind their playful demeanor lies a genetic risk for Pyruvate Kinase Deficiency (PKDefef), a hereditary condition causing red blood cell destruction and severe anemia, alongside Progressive Retinal Atrophy (PRA). Additionally, their silky semi-longhair coat requires regular grooming to prevent hairballs and matting. In this guide, decodes Somali health signals and outlines a 3-step daily care routine.
