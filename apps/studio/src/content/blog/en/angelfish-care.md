Renowned for their tall, elegant fins and regal swimming motion, the Angelfish (Pterophyllum scalare) is a quintessential medium-sized cichlid in the freshwater aquarium hobby. Due to their tall, vertically extended fins, providing a deep aquarium (water depth of 45cm or more) is essential. Poor water quality or rising ammonia levels easily lead to Fin Rot or 'Hole-in-the-Head' disease (Hexamita). Furthermore, as adult cichlids, they harbor a natural predatory instinct to eat small fish (such as neon tetras) that fit into their mouths. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Angelfish's body language and maintain pristine water quality.
