Renowned for their tall, elegant fins and regal swimming motion, the Angelfish (Pterophyllum scalare) is a quintessential medium-sized cichlid in the freshwater aquarium hobby. Due to their tall, vertically extended fins, providing a deep aquarium (water depth of 45cm or more) is essential. Poor water quality or rising ammonia levels easily lead to Fin Rot or 'Hole-in-the-Head' disease (Hexamita). Furthermore, as adult cichlids, they harbor a natural predatory instinct to eat small fish (such as neon tetras) that fit into their mouths. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Angelfish's body language and maintain pristine water quality.

## 2. Angelfish (Pterophyllum scalare)'s Body Language & Behavioral Signals

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">Body Signal 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Interlocking Mouths and Pushing Head-on (Jaw-Locking / Lip-Locking)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A social strength test used to establish dominance, resolve territorial disputes, or confirm pair bonding during breeding.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Provide aquarium decorations or tall plants to break line of sight, preventing severe injuries or fin nipping.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">

  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A sign of severe environmental stress caused by ammonia/nitrite spikes, pH shock, or internal Hexamita infection.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Test water parameters immediately for ammonia and pH, performing a 20–30% partial water change with dechlorinated water.
