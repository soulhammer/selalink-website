## 1. Protecting Brain's Finite Glucose Resources and Preventing Ego Depletion

The energy used by the prefrontal cortex for decision making is finite. As decisions pile up, brain cells consume glucose and oxygen, suffering from 'ego depletion' which weakens executive control. Trivial choices like 'what to wear this morning' or 'what to eat for lunch' drain the brain's executive budget. Automating daily routines and uniforming clothes allows the prefrontal cortex to preserve glucose budgets for key business decisions or long-term strategies, vastly improving the quality of each decision.
