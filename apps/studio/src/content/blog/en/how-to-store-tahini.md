Have you ever opened a jar of tahini to make hummus or dressing, only to find liquid oil floating on top and a rock-hard layer at the bottom? 🫙

Tahini, made from finely ground roasted sesame seeds, is a suspension of solid particles suspended in sesame oil. Over time, gravity induces natural lipid-solid phase separation: clear sesame oil floats to the top while solid particles settle densely at the bottom. Fortunately, sesame seeds contain powerful natural antioxidants—sesamol and sesamolin—giving tahini exceptional resistance to lipid oxidation.

Based on official guidelines from the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) and the USDA, this 3-step scientific storage guide explains how to prevent solid hardening and keep tahini smooth and fresh for over a year. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Periodically flip unopened jars upside down at room temperature — Sealed Pantry Flipping (180–365 Days) 🫙</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Sesamol in sesame seeds resists rancidity even at room temperature before opening. 💡 Storage Tip: Flip the jar upside down every 1–2 weeks to reverse gravitational force and naturally prevent hard sediment compaction.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Stir thoroughly after opening and refrigerate — Cold Storage (365+ Days) ❄️</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not leave jars stationary to prevent bottom solids from hardening ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Leaving a jar of tahini in one position for months allows gravity to pack solid particles into a clay-like mass at the bottom, making re-emulsification difficult. Turn the jar upside down periodically.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Prevent moisture intrusion and foreign contamination completely 🛑</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      While low moisture in pure tahini prevents bacterial growth, introducing water from wet spoons seizes the paste, ruining its texture and inviting bacterial or fungal spoilage.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/60 dark:border-white/10 dark:bg-slate-900/40 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">

      <span class="pr-4">How can I fix tahini that has hardened solidly at the bottom of the jar?</span>
