Fish cakes (eomuk) are a beloved, versatile ingredient used in soups, stir-fries, braises, and tteokbokki. But have you ever placed a package of fish cakes in the fridge only to find them slimy with an off-odor just a few days later? 🍢

Fish cakes have high moisture and protein content, and their surfaces are coated in frying oil. When exposed to oxygen, surface lipids oxidize rapidly, creating an ideal breeding ground for spoilage bacteria. This is why their refrigerated shelf life is very short (3 to 5 days). In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step fish cake storage guide based on expert guidelines to remove surface oil and extend their fresh taste and chewy texture for up to 1 to 2 months. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Consume within 3 to 5 days when refrigerated after opening ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Because fish cakes have high moisture activity and surface oil, spoilage bacteria and Listeria monocytogenes can grow even at refrigeration temperatures (0–4°C). Consume opened fish cakes quickly within 3 to 5 days.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Thoroughly remove moisture after blanching before freezing 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Freezing blanched fish cakes while still wet causes excessive ice crystal formation, breaking down the fish protein gel structure and leaving them spongy upon thawing. Cool, drain, and dry completely before freezing.
    </p>
  </div>
