Have you ever put an unripe avocado in the refrigerator only to have its flesh turn black, stringy, and mealy? 🥑

Avocados are climacteric fruits that continue to respire and ripen after being harvested. Storing them at the wrong temperature can permanently halt ripening or destroy their internal cellular structure, requiring special care.

In this guide, shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on official recommendations from the California Avocado Commission (CAC) and the USDA to maximize freshness and preserve flavor and nutrients. 🛡️

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Store in a paper bag at room temperature before fully ripe — Room Temp Storage (5 Days) 🥑</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never refrigerate unripe avocados ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Exposing hard, green avocados to low temperatures (below 7°C / 45°F) causes chilling injury. Cellular tissue breaks down, permanently stopping the ripening process and leaving the flesh black, stringy, and mealy even if moved back to room temperature.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Check for deep black spots and severe softness 🥑</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      If large black spots cover the skin and the fruit feels excessively soft when lightly squeezed, the flesh inside has likely rotted. Blackened vascular strands running through the flesh also indicate spoilage, so exercise caution before consuming.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
