Have you ever put an unripe avocado in the refrigerator only to have its flesh turn black, stringy, and mealy? 🥑

Avocados are climacteric fruits that continue to respire and ripen after being harvested. Storing them at the wrong temperature can permanently halt ripening or destroy their internal cellular structure, requiring special care.

Today, FreshSnap shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on official recommendations from the California Avocado Commission (CAC) and the USDA to maximize freshness and preserve flavor and nutrients. 🛡️

## 1. 3-Step Storage Method

    Firm, unripe avocados must be kept at a room temperature of 15–20°C (59–68°F) to ripen normally. 💡 Storage Tip: Place avocados in a paper bag with an apple or banana. The ethylene gas released by these fruits concentrates inside the bag, accelerating and stabilizing the avocado's ripening process.

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Never refrigerate unripe avocados ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Exposing hard, green avocados to low temperatures (below 7°C / 45°F) causes chilling injury. Cellular tissue breaks down, permanently stopping the ripening process and leaving the flesh black, stringy, and mealy even if moved back to room temperature.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Check for deep black spots and severe softness 🥑</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      If large black spots cover the skin and the fruit feels excessively soft when lightly squeezed, the flesh inside has likely rotted. Blackened vascular strands running through the flesh also indicate spoilage, so exercise caution before consuming.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
