## 1. Target Framing Effect of Mandalart and Neuroscientific Recovery Mechanisms of Long Sleep

The Mandalart technique, which breaks abstract goals into concrete actions, blocks cognitive overload and stimulates the brain's execution pathways. Meanwhile, Ohtani's signature 10-hour sleep window activates the brain's glymphatic system. Cerebrospinal fluid clears accumulated metabolic waste, restoring the prefrontal cortex's cognitive energy battery. Additionally, sleep safely registers the motor neural patterns of pitching and batting practiced that day into the cerebellum's long-term motor memory storage.
