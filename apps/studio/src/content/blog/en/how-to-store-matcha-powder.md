Have you ever opened a pouch of matcha powder only to find its vivid green color dulled to a brownish hue and its aromatic freshness gone? 🍵

Matcha powder is made by stone-grinding shade-grown tea leaves into ultra-fine particles. This creates an extremely large surface area exposed to oxygen and light. As a result, its key antioxidants—catechins (EGCG)—and vibrant green chlorophyll rapidly undergo photo-oxidation when exposed to UV light, ambient light, and air, destroying nutrients and browning the powder. Furthermore, matcha exhibits high hygroscopicity, quickly absorbing atmospheric moisture which causes clumping and mold growth.

In this guide, we share a scientifically proven 3-step matcha powder storage guide based on standards from MAFF (Japan), USDA, and MFDS to preserve its bright green color, umami flavor, and active nutrients. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Strongly shield from light (UV) and oxygen exposure ☀️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Because ultra-fine matcha particles have a massive surface area, light and oxygen trigger rapid photo-oxidation, degrading catechins (EGCG) and chlorophyll into a dull brown. Always store in a light-proof, opaque airtight container (such as a dark tin or aluminum pouch).
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Beware of moisture absorption causing clumping and mold growth 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Matcha powder is highly hygroscopic and rapidly absorbs ambient moisture. Dampness leads to particle clumping and creates a breeding ground for microorganisms. Always use completely dry utensils.
    </p>
