Have you ever stored fresh chestnuts at room temperature only to find them covered in white mold, shriveled, or infested with weevil larvae within a few days? 🌰

Unlike most nuts that are rich in fats, chestnuts have a unique composition containing about 50% water and a high starch content. Storing them at room temperature causes rapid moisture loss, shrinkage, Fusarium and Penicillium mold growth, and activation of chestnut weevil larvae.

In this guide, based on official guidelines from the USDA and the Rural Development Administration (RDA), we share a 3-step scientific storage method to prevent moisture loss, stop mold growth, and maximize sweetness through 'chilling saccharification.' 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Store at 0–2°C for cold saccharification ripening — Cold Saccharification Storage (30–60 Days) 🌰</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Storing chestnuts at 0–2°C (32–36°F) and 85–90% relative humidity activates amylase enzymes. This triggers 'chilling saccharification,' converting starch into glucose and sugars to maximize sweetness. 💡 Storage Tip: Soak in saltwater for 1–2 hours, discard floating nuts, dry surfaces thoroughly, and store in perforated bags at 0–2°C.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Filter floating infested nuts using saltwater immersion — Saltwater Screening 🧂</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Submerging chestnuts in saltwater causes hollow or insect-damaged nuts to float to the surface. 💡 Storage Tip: Skim off and discard floating nuts. Wash remaining good chestnuts, dry surfaces completely in the shade, and proceed to cold storage to block mold growth.
  </p>
</div>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Parboil or steam before peeling and freezing for long-term storage — Parboiled Freezing (180–360 Days) 🧊</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never leave chestnuts at room temperature ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Chestnuts contain nearly 50% water. Exposing them to room temperature (above 15°C / 59°F) leads to rapid water evaporation, flesh shrinkage, Penicillium mold infection, and weevil infestation within 3 to 5 days.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not seal while wet or refrigerate completely dry 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Sealing chestnuts with excess surface water promotes fungal decay, whereas keeping them completely exposed causes shriveling. Soak in saltwater to filter damaged nuts, thoroughly dry the surface, and store in perforated plastic bags or with paper towels.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
