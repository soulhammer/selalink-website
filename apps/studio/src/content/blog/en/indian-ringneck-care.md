The Indian Ringneck Parakeet is a medium-sized parrot wildly popular on social media for the adult male's distinguished neck ring and remarkable speech mimicry skills. Highly intelligent and independent, they go through a challenging juvenile 'bluffing' phase around one year of age characterized by sudden biting and aggression. Overcoming this phase requires consistent behavioral training, speech engagement, and a pellet-centered diet balanced with fresh produce. In this guide, decodes Indian Ringneck behavioral signals and shares a 3-step homecare routine for vocal bonding, bluffing correction, and optimal nutrition.
