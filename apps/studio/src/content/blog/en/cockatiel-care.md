Characterized by bright orange cheeks and an elegant head crest, the Cockatiel is one of the most cherished companion birds globally. Cockatiels form deep emotional bonds with human caregivers and love whistling tunes, yet they retain wild prey instincts that make them highly susceptible to 'Night Frights'—sudden nocturnal panic attacks triggered by dim shadows or distant noises. Guided by Association of Avian Veterinarians (AAV) protocols, this guide breaks down their emotional body language and establishes a daily routine for optimal health.
