Native to the freshwater rivers of South America's Amazon basin, the Corydoras Catfish ('Cory') is a beloved bottom-dwelling armored catfish cherished by aquarists worldwide for its cute barbels and industrious bottom-scanning swimming style. While famously peaceful, Corydoras catfish possess delicate sensory barbels around their mouths that easily erode or develop bacterial infections if kept on sharp gravel or dirty substrates. Furthermore, they are highly sensitive to nitrogenous waste buildup (ammonia and nitrite) and possess a specialized physiological mechanism for 'intestinal respiration,' darting to the surface to gulp air when dissolved oxygen drops. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Corydoras' body language and protect its health.

## 2. Corydoras Catfish (Corydoras)'s Body Language & Behavioral Signals

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">Body Signal 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Darting Rapidly to the Water Surface to Gulp Air (Surface Gulping / Intestinal Respiration)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A natural physiological adaptation for oxygen intake, but excessive frequency indicates hypoxia or poor filtration.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Occasional gulping is normal; if frequent, immediately increase aeration/bubblers and test ammonia/nitrite levels.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">Body Signal 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Holding Fins Clamped Against Body or Pacing Glass Walls Up and Down (Clamped Fins & Glass Surfing)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> Signals severe water contamination, sudden temperature drops, or bacterial skin/barbel irritation.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Check temperature (22–26°C), perform a 20–30% water change with conditioned water, and vacuum substrate detritus.
  </p>
</div>
