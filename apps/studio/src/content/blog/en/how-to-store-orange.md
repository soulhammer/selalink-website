Have you ever bought a box of oranges only to find their skins shreveeled and dry, or covered in blue-green mold after a few days? 🍊

Oranges are rich in moisture and vitamin C, but as citrus fruits, they continue to respire and release water even after harvesting. Leaving them at room temperature causes moisture loss that makes the flesh soft and tasteless, while trapped humidity rapidly breeds green and blue molds that infect neighboring fruit.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step orange storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maximize freshness. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Wrap individually in paper towels or newspaper — Moisture Retention & Mold Barrier 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Direct contact between oranges causes friction and traps moisture, accelerating spoilage. 💡 Storage Tip: Wrap each unwashed orange tightly in a paper towel or newspaper. This traps moisture inside the fruit while preventing mold spores from spreading.
  </p>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Store in cold refrigeration at 4–8°C — Cold Storage (3–4 Weeks) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    The optimal temperature to preserve orange freshness for a long time is 4–8°C (39–46°F). 💡 Storage Tip: Place wrapped oranges inside a perforated plastic bag or container in the refrigerator crisper drawer. This slows respiration, preserving juicy sweetness for 3 to 4 weeks.
  </p>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Peel and segment before freezing for long-term storage — Frozen Storage (2–3 Months) ❄️</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never wash oranges before storing 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Moisture left on the orange peel creates an ideal environment for mold spores to thrive. Wipe off surface dirt or dust with a dry cloth or paper towel before storage, and wash oranges right before eating.
    </p>
  </div>
