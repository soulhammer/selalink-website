Louis Braille, the inventor who revolutionized literacy for the visually impaired. How did he create the 6-dot braille system? ⠃

Braille realized the 12-dot military code was too large to read with one fingertip. Testing tactile sensations daily, he stripped away complexity until he reached the optimal 6-dot system. Removing clutter was his ultimate strength.

Today, BuildSelf introduces Braille's 6-dot simplification routine.

## 1. Preventing Cognitive Overload via Working Memory Chunking

Human working memory has strict limits on information processed at once.

Just as Braille cut 12 dots down to 6 for instant recognition, <strong>'chunking'</strong> complex processes into 3-5 core items eliminates cognitive load and boosts speed.
