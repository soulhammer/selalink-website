Have you ever bought a fresh bundle of cilantro only to find its stems wilted and leaves yellowing within days? 🌿

Cilantro is a popular aromatic herb used globally, but its thin leaf cells undergo rapid transpiration. Exposure to ambient temperatures causes a quick drop in cell turgor pressure, leading to wilting. Additionally, cilantro is highly sensitive to ethylene gas, which triggers rapid chlorophyll degradation (yellowing).

Based on official guidelines from the UC Davis Postharvest Technology Center and USDA, this 3-step scientific storage guide demonstrates how hydroponic cold storage preserves cilantro's aroma and crispness for up to 3 weeks. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Trim stem bottoms and store upright in a jar of water — Hydroponic Supply (14–21 Days) 🌿</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">

  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Loosely cover leaf top with a plastic bag and refrigerate — Cold Humidity Control (1–4°C) ❄️</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Room temperature exposure and soggy leaves cause rapid wilting and decay ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Leaving cilantro at room temperature accelerates transpiration, leading to a loss of cell turgor pressure and wilting within hours. Sealing cilantro with excess surface water also speeds up bacterial rot.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Isolate from ethylene-producing fruits 🛑</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Cilantro is extremely sensitive to ethylene gas. Storing it near fruits like apples, bananas, or tomatoes destroys chlorophyll, turning leaves yellow and shortening shelf life.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
