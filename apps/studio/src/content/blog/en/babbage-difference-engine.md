"Errors originate from human emotion, and truth is perfected in mechanical precision." ⚙️

Charles Babbage (1791–1871), the great 19th-century British mathematician, mechanical engineer, and father of modern computers. He was a pioneer of the information revolution who blueprinted the Difference Engine, the world's first automatic mechanical calculator, and the Analytical Engine, the precursor to modern programmable computers.

In the 1820s, while examining nautical tables for the Royal Astronomical Society, Babbage witnessed tragedies where ships ran aground due to numerous errors and mistakes made by human calculators. Confronted with this problem, Babbage established an unmatched thinking habit of decomposing vast, complex computational blueprints into hundreds of independent mechanical modules of cogs and gears one by one. His cry—"I wish to God these calculations had been executed by steam and mechanical gears without error rather than human emotion!"—became a reality.

This habit of dismantling massive dilemmas into minimal cog modules where errors can no longer occur, sequentially aligning input-output causal relationships, served as the pinnacle of informatics and neuroscience in preventing brain working memory overload and refining systemic error to 0%.

## 1. Why System Modularization Eliminates Prefrontal Working Memory Overload

According to cognitive science research, human Working Memory can only process 4 to 7 information chunks at a time. Decomposing complex dilemmas into independent cog modules prevents prefrontal cortex overload and fatigue, controlling errors down near 0%.

## 3. Precautions for System Modularization Alignment to Prevent Mechanical Errors

Do not randomly mix and execute the order of decomposed modules. You must strictly align causal relationships so that the output of the preceding module connects seamlessly into the input of the next module. Rather than recklessly relying on willpower alone, designing a physical environment and habit cues in advance where action is naturally triggered is the core of sustainability.
