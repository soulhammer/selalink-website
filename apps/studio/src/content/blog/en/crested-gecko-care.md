Famous for the eyelash-like crests above their eyes, the Crested Gecko (Correlophus ciliatus) is one of the most beloved pet reptiles worldwide due to its gentle nature and convenient powdered diet (Superfood / Meal Replacement Powder). As an arboreal reptile, providing a tall enclosure, proper humidity cycles (60%–80%), and cool temperatures (20°C–26°C) is essential. Preventing Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD) caused by calcium deficiency is crucial, and since a dropped tail does not regenerate, avoiding stress is a priority. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Crested Gecko's body signals and maintain its health.
