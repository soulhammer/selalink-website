Budgerigars (Budgies) are among the most popular pet birds worldwide, cherished for their vibrant plumage, cheerful chirping, and affectionate nature. However, due to their unique avian respiratory system with air sacs, they are extremely vulnerable to airborne toxins like PTFE (Teflon) fumes, perfumes, and aerosol sprays. Furthermore, an all-seed diet often leads to hepatic lipidosis (fatty liver disease) and severe nutritional deficiencies. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to understand your budgie's body language, ensure respiratory safety, and maintain a balanced diet.
