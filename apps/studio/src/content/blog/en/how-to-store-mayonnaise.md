Have you ever left an opened jar or bottle of mayonnaise in the back of your fridge, only to find the oil and water split into greasy layers? 🧴

Mayonnaise is a classic emulsion made by precisely combining vegetable oil, egg yolk, and vinegar or lemon juice. It is delicate to temperature changes—especially freezing temperatures, which ruin the emulsion and cause oil separation.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step mayonnaise storage guide based on official guidelines from the FDA, USDA, and MFDS to keep your mayonnaise rich, smooth, and fresh after opening. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Pantry before opening, refrigerate immediately after — Refrigerated Storage (60–90 Days) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Unopened mayonnaise can be kept in a cool, dry pantry away from sunlight, but opening it exposes the product to oxygen and microbes. 💡 Storage Tip: Always refrigerate mayonnaise once opened and consume it within 2–3 months (60–90 days) for optimal safety and flavor.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Store at 4–8°C on door shelves or main shelves — Maintain Optimal Refrigeration Temperature 🌡️</h4>
  </div>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never freeze mayonnaise or place it near freezing vents ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      When mayonnaise drops below 0°C (32°F), the bond between oil, egg yolk, and vinegar breaks, leading to emulsion breakdown and oil separation. Once separated, mayonnaise cannot be re-emulsified, so keep it away from sub-zero zones and direct cold air vents.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Wipe the nozzle clean and seal tightly after use 🧴</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Leftover mayonnaise around the bottle lip oxidizes quickly upon contact with air and can invite mold or bacterial growth. Always wipe the cap or nozzle with a clean tissue immediately after use and seal it tightly.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
