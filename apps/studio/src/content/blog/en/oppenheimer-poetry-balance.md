J. Robert Oppenheimer, the theoretical physicist who led the Manhattan Project. How did he handle extreme pressure? 📜

Every night, Oppenheimer stepped away from complex physics equations and read Sanskrit poetry in its original language. Immersing himself in poetic analog language served as his ultimate 'cognitive balance switch'.

Today, BuildSelf introduces Oppenheimer's poetry routine.

## 1. Heterogeneous Shift Awaking Right-Brain Emotional Circuits

Prolonged exposure to numbers degrades cognitive flexibility due to left-brain overload.

Reading poetry triggers a <strong>'heterogeneous cognitive shift'</strong>, relaxing mathematical cortex overload and lowering stress hormones.
