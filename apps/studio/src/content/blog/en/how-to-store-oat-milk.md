Oat milk has become a widely popular plant-based milk alternative known for its nutty and smooth flavor! 🥛🌾

Oats are rich in 'beta-glucan,' a soluble dietary fiber, along with natural starches, making oat milk a highly nutritious plant-based beverage. However, storage rules for oat milk differ drastically before and after opening. Unopened aseptic UHT packages can be kept at room temperature. Once opened, however, exposure to air introduces spoilage microorganisms and bacteria like Pseudomonas, which can multiply rapidly.

In this guide, based on scientifically verified guidelines from the USDA, FDA, and MFDS, we present a 3-step optimal storage strategy to preserve the nutrient content and fresh flavor of oat milk. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never leave opened oat milk at room temperature ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Unlike sealed shelf-stable packs, opened oat milk is rich in proteins and starches, creating an ideal breeding ground for airborne bacteria like Pseudomonas spp. Leaving it at room temperature can cause spoilage within 2 hours; always refrigerate immediately after use.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not freeze oat milk in the freezer 🧊</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Freezing oat milk breaks its water-and-fat emulsion while destroying the gelled structure of beta-glucan and starches. Upon thawing, the liquid separates into watery and grainy phases, resulting in a slimy, unpleasant texture.
    </p>
  </div>
