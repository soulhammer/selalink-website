Renowned for its vivid neon blue stripe and full-length brilliant red belly, the Cardinal Tetra (Paracheirodon axelrodi) is a quintessential freshwater schooling fish. Unlike the similar Neon Tetra, whose red stripe covers only the rear half, the Cardinal Tetra's belly is completely saturated red. However, they are more sensitive to water parameters, requiring soft, slightly acidic water (pH 5.5–6.8) and low nitrates. They also depend on schooling in groups to feel secure and can lose color under severe stress or disease. In this guide, breaks down Cardinal Tetra signals and outlines a 3-step daily homecare routine.
