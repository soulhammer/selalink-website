George Washington, the hero who led the American Revolutionary War to victory and guided the young country. Under massive administration duties, the secret behind his analytical power was his early farm management routine. ☀️

Washington woke up at 4 AM from his youth to his later years. He rode a horse to inspect Mount Vernon. Returning to his study, he recorded temperature, wind, crops, and fertilizer data in a leather logbook manually. This early morning routine of decision-making based on precise figures was the foundation of his administrative power.

Today, BuildSelf looks at the science behind Washington's logbook routine and how modern people can activate metacognition to control business and daily metrics.

## 1. How Meticulous Metric Recording Awakens Metacognition

Metacognition is the ability to accurately distinguish what you know and what you do not. If metacognition is weak, you rely on superficial senses and make wrong decisions without realizing inefficiency. Recording actual figures manually, like Washington, highly activates the monitoring area of the prefrontal cortex, filtering cognitive illusions and guiding error-free resource allocation decisions.
