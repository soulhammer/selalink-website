Famous globally through the legendary story of Hachiko, the Akita Inu is Japan's premier large breed, revered for its stoic dignity and fierce loyalty. Built for cold, mountainous origins, they possess a dense double coat that experiences massive seasonal shedding ('coat blowouts'). Highly intelligent and self-reliant, Akitas require consistent, assertive leadership and extensive early socialization from puppyhood to prevent dog-on-dog aggression and territorial overprotectiveness. Today, PetSelf decodes Akita behavioral signals and presents a 3-step homecare routine for coat maintenance, authority training, and cool temperature management.
