Have you ever bought crisp celery only to find it limp, rubbery, or dried out after just a few days in the fridge? 🥬

Celery is composed of nearly 95% water, making it extremely vulnerable to moisture loss and wilting. Storing celery in tight plastic bags traps ethylene gas and excessive humidity, causing it to spoil and become soft prematurely.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step celery storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maximize crispness for up to 4 weeks. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not store celery in sealed plastic bags ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Sealed plastic bags trap moisture and naturally emitted ethylene gas, accelerating decay and mold growth. Using aluminum foil allows ethylene to dissipate while retaining optimal humidity.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never freeze raw celery whole without blanching 🧊</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Because of its high water content, freezing raw celery forms ice crystals that break down cell walls, rendering it mushy upon thawing. Always blanch celery briefly in boiling water before freezing.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
