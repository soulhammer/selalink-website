Vampire Crabs (*Geosesarma dennerle*, *Geosesarma hagen*, etc.) are small, semi-terrestrial land crabs famed worldwide among paludarium and vivarium enthusiasts for their striking yellow eyes and brilliant purple or orange carapaces. Native to the tropical rainforests of Southeast Asia, they thrive in high-humidity enclosures with a land-to-water ratio of roughly 80:20. Unlike land hermit crabs, Vampire Crabs are fully freshwater species that complete their entire lifecycle—including hatching fully formed spiderlings from eggs—without needing saltwater. High humidity (75–85%), calcium nutrition for molting, and ample hides to reduce male territorial conflict are essential. In this guide, breaks down Vampire Crab behaviors and offers a 3-step daily care routine.
