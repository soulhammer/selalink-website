Soy milk is a fragrant, nutrient-rich plant beverage enjoyed by people of all ages! 🥛🫘

Extracted from soybeans, soy milk is packed with plant-based protein, healthy fats, and moisture, making it a cornerstone healthy drink. While unopened aseptic UHT packages can be kept at room temperature, opening the container creates an environment with high water activity (Aw) and rich nutrients ideal for bacterial growth, such as coliform bacteria and Bacillus cereus. If left above 10°C (50°F), acidity rises rapidly, causing protein coagulation where the liquid clumps into curds like soft tofu.

In this guide, based on scientifically verified guidelines from the USDA, FDA, and MFDS, we present a 3-step optimal storage strategy to preserve the nutty flavor and rich nutrients of soy milk. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never leave opened soy milk at room temperature above 10°C ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Soy milk is rich in protein and fat with high water activity. Leaving it above 10°C (50°F) causes coliform bacteria and Bacillus cereus to multiply rapidly. As bacteria produce acid, rising acidity causes proteins to coagulate into curd-like lumps. Always refrigerate immediately after use.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not freeze soy milk in its container 🧊</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Freezing soy milk disrupts the emulsion structure of fat and protein particles, completely separating water from solids. Upon thawing, the texture becomes grainy and watery, destroying its smooth, creamy flavor.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
