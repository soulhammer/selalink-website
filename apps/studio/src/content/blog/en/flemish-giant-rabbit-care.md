Reaching adult weights of 6 to over 10 kg, the Flemish Giant is the largest domesticated rabbit breed in the world. Often dubbed the 'Gentle Giant' or the 'Maine Coon of rabbits,' they are renowned for their calm, dog-like affection. Standard rabbit cages are entirely unsuitable for their massive size, making large exercise pens or indoor free-roaming essential. Furthermore, supplying unlimited hay to wear down continuously growing teeth and preventing joint strain and pododermatitis (sore hocks) are critical for their longevity. Today on PetSelf, we translate Flemish Giant instinctual signals and present a 3-step homecare routine for indoor roaming, gastrointestinal/dental wellness, and paw pad protection.
