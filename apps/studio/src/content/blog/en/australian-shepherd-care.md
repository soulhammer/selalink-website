Renowned for their striking coat variations, exceptional intelligence, and boundless energy, the Australian Shepherd ('Aussie') is a world-class canine athlete and devoted family companion. Highly loyal and eager to learn, Aussies possess a powerful herding instinct that may manifest as ankle-nipping toward running children or other pets. Furthermore, they carry a high prevalence of the MDR1 (Multi-Drug Resistance 1) gene mutation, making them extremely sensitive to certain routine veterinary medications. They are also prone to hereditary ocular disorders such as Collie Eye Anomaly (CEA) and Hip Dysplasia (HD). Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Aussie's body language and protect its health.
