Famous for their round bodies and oversized mouths resembling the classic arcade character, the Pacman Frog (Ceratophrys) is a beloved terrestrial amphibian kept worldwide. As sedentary ambush predators that bury themselves in substrate waiting for prey, Pacman Frogs possess voracious appetites. However, they are extremely vulnerable to intestinal impaction caused by ingesting substrate or oversized prey. Furthermore, because amphibians possess semi-permeable skin that directly absorbs water, electrolytes, and toxins, poor water quality or bacterial contamination can cause fatal Red Leg Disease and toxic shock. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Pacman Frog's body language and protect its health.
