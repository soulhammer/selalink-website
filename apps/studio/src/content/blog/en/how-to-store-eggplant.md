Have you ever bought fresh eggplants only to find their skins wrinkled or their flesh turned brown and seedy within just a few days? 🍆

As warm-climate produce, eggplants are extremely cold-sensitive, soft-textured, and hyper-responsive to temperature fluctuations, moisture loss, and ethylene gas. Storing raw eggplants at standard refrigerator temperatures (0–4°C) causes severe chilling injury, resulting in pitting, internal browning, soft texture, and a bitter taste.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step eggplant storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maximize freshness and tender texture. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Wrap individually with paper towels or newspaper — Retain Moisture & Cushion Cold Air 🧻</h4>

  </p>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not expose directly to cold temperatures below 7°C (Prevent Chilling Injury) ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The optimal storage temperature for eggplants is 10–12°C (50–54°F). Storing them in the coldest spots of a refrigerator causes surface pitting, internal discoloration, and increased bitterness due to chilling injury.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Isolate from ethylene-producing fruits like apples and tomatoes 🍎</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Eggplants are highly sensitive to ethylene gas. Storing them near apples, bananas, or tomatoes causes rapid softening, internal browning, and accelerated decay.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/60 dark:border-white/10 dark:bg-slate-900/40 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
