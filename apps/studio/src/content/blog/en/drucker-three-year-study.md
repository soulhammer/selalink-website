## 1. Why Studying a New Field Keeps the Brain Young

A brain that only uses the same routine and knowledge is prone to aging. When studying a new discipline or an unfamiliar field, the brain activates synapses, new connections between neurons, instead of familiar neural pathways. This is called <strong>neuroplasticity</strong>. Through this process, the brain remains intellectually young.
