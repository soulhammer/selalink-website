Chihuahuas, originating from Mexico, are the smallest dogs in the world but boast a courageous, lion-like personality. Known for their large ears, big eyes, and proud steps, they are fiercely loyal to their owner. 🐕

However, their tiny frames come with serious structural vulnerabilities. First is the Molera (open fontanelle), an opening in their skull bones. Pressure on their forehead translates directly to the brain, requiring special handling. Also, they have minimal body fat and high metabolic rates, making them susceptible to shivering and Hypoglycemia (low blood sugar) during long fasting periods.

Today, PetSelf decodes Chihuahua body language and introduces a precise 3-step home care routine for molera protection and metabolic health.
