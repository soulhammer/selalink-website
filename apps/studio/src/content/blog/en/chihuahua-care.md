Chihuahuas, originating from Mexico, are the smallest dogs in the world but boast a courageous, lion-like personality as true 'little warriors'. Known for their large ears, big round eyes, and proud steps, they are fiercely loyal to their owner. 🐕

However, their tiny frames come with serious structural vulnerabilities. First is the Molera (open fontanelle), an opening in their skull bones. Pressure on their forehead translates directly to the brain, requiring special handling to prevent impacts. Also, they have minimal body fat and high metabolic rates, making them susceptible to shivering and Hypoglycemia (low blood sugar shock) during long fasting periods.

In this guide, decodes Chihuahua body language and introduces a precise 3-step homecare routine for molera protection and metabolic health.
