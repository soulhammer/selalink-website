## 1. Enhancing Episodic Memory Circuits via Fine Sensory Logging

In daily monotony, the brain routinely erases familiar information.

Recording specific sensory details of exotic places nightly dramatically strengthens <strong>episodic memory</strong> circuits in the hippocampus.
