Have you ever taken out buckwheat flour or noodles only to find that their signature nutty aroma was gone, replaced by a bitter, rancid odor? 🌾

Buckwheat is a highly nutritious pseudograin rich in the antioxidant rutin and a high proportion of unsaturated fatty acids. However, once milled into flour, exposure to air and moisture rapidly activates the enzyme lipase. Lipase breaks down unsaturated fatty acids into free fatty acids, destroying buckwheat's pleasant nutty flavor and causing bitterness and rancidity.

Based on official guidelines from the Rural Development Administration (RDA) and the USDA, this 3-step scientific storage guide explains how to inhibit lipase activity and preserve buckwheat's flavor for over a year. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Store in airtight containers below 4°C — Cold Refrigerated Storage (180 Days) 🌾</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    Keep buckwheat flour (moisture content ≤ 13%) in an airtight container under 50% relative humidity inside the fridge (0–4°C / 32–39°F). 💡 Storage Tip: Temperatures below 4°C strongly suppress lipase activity, locking in freshness for 6 months.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Freeze in airtight packaging for long-term storage — Frozen Storage (365+ Days) 🧊</h4>
  </div>

  </p>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Room temperature and moisture exposure trigger lipase activity and rancidity ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Leaving buckwheat flour in warm and humid conditions sharply boosts lipase activity, rapidly decomposing fatty acids. This degrades the nutty flavor and produces a harsh bitter taste.
    </p>
  </div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">

  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Block direct sunlight and oxygen contact completely 🛑</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">

    </p>
