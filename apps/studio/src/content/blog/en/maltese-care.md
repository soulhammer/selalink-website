Maltese dogs, with their white, silky coat and three adorable black dots (eyes and nose), are long-cherished companion dogs around the world. While lively and affectionate, they express their boundaries clearly, earning them a cute 'spunky' reputation. 🐕

However, Maltese owners often worry about two critical genetic issues: Patellar Luxation (dislocated kneecaps) and Tear Staining (epiphora), which leaves red-brown streaks on white fur and easily triggers facial dermatitis. Daily lifestyle protective routines are crucial for managing these conditions.

In this guide, we explore Maltese body language and introduces a safe, 3-step daily care routine to protect their joints and eyes.
