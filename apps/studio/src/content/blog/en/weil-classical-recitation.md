## 1. Vocal Recitation and Prefrontal Cortex Activation

Reading complex classical texts aloud activates the Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex (DLPFC), the center of cognitive control and working memory. While silent reading mostly uses visual circuits, reading aloud demands visual, motor, and auditory systems to work simultaneously. This multisensory integration synchronizes brain waves, dramatically improving selective attention and clearing mental fog for the rest of the day.
