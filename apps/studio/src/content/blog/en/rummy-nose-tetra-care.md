The Rummy-Nose Tetra (Hemigrammus bleheri) is an iconic South American characin prized by aquascapers worldwide for its vibrant ruby-red head, black-and-white zebra-striped tail, and unmatched tight-schooling behavior. Highly sensitive to water parameters, the intense red coloration on their head serves as a living barometer for water quality (nitrates, pH) and overall health. Furthermore, they thrive and display their famous synchronized swimming when kept in schools of 10 or more. In this guide, we analyze the Rummy-Nose Tetra's behavioral and health signals and share a 3-step daily routine for vibrant head coloration, water stability, and perfect schooling harmony.
