Have you ever brought home crisp fresh basil, put it in the refrigerator crisper drawer, and found it turned completely black and wilted the next day? 🌿

Basil is a tropical and subtropical herb that is extremely sensitive to cold temperatures. When exposed to temperatures below 10°C (50°F)—such as a standard refrigerator at ~4°C—it suffers from 'chilling injury,' causing its cell membranes to collapse and the leaves to blacken rapidly.

In this guide, based on scientific guidelines from the USDA and the UC Davis Postharvest Technology Center, we share a 3-step storage method to maintain fresh basil's vibrant aroma and freshness. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never store fresh basil in temperatures below 10°C (50°F) ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Exposing fresh basil to temperatures below 10°C breaks down cellular tissue and activates polyphenol oxidase (PPO) enzymes, rapidly browning the leaves. Within hours, leaves become soft and lose essential aromatic compounds like linalool and eugenol.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not store with excess moisture on the leaves 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Standing moisture on leaf surfaces accelerates fungal and bacterial growth and leads to rapid rotting. Wash basil only immediately before cooking and ensure leaves are dry during storage.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
