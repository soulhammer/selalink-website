Have you ever placed bell peppers in the refrigerator only to find them watery, soft, and pitted after a few days? 🫑

Bell peppers consist of over 90% water, making them highly sensitive to moisture and cold temperatures. Storing them below 7°C triggers 'chilling injury,' causing cell walls to break down, resulting in surface pitting and soft rot. Excess surface moisture also breeds mold around the stem.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step bell pepper storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maximize crispness and sweetness. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Wipe dry and wrap individually in paper towels — Moisture Control 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Managing moisture is key to preventing decay. 💡 Storage Tip: Do not wash. Gently wipe off surface dampness with a dry cloth or paper towel, then wrap each bell pepper individually in a paper towel with the stem facing up.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Seal in plastic wrap and store in the crisper drawer — Cold Storage (10–14 Days) ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Wrapping prevents moisture loss while blocking external contaminants. 💡 Storage Tip: Place paper-wrapped peppers inside sealed plastic wrap or a Ziploc bag, and store in the refrigerator crisper drawer (7–10°C) to maintain crispness for 10 to 14 days.
  </p>
</div>

  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Do not wash bell peppers before storing 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Water trapped around the stem or skin crevices creates a breeding ground for mold and bacteria. Keep bell peppers dry and wipe away any dirt with a paper towel before storage; wash only right before cooking.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Avoid storing below 5°C to prevent chilling injury ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Bell peppers are cold-sensitive crops. Prolonged exposure to temperatures below 5°C causes pitting (sunken spots) and breakdown of cell tissue, leading to moisture loss. Store them in the warmer crisper drawer (7–10°C).
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
