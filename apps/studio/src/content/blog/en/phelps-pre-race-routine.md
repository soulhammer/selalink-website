## 1. Science of How High-Precision Pre-Routines Calm Brain Anxiety

The most dangerous state the brain can face before a race or presentation is 'uncertainty' and resulting anxiety. In an unpredictable environment, the brain's emotion and alarm center, the <strong>prefrontal cortex</strong> (Wait, should be prefrontal cortex or amygdala? Let's use amygdala), the <strong>amygdala</strong>, overheats and releases stress hormones. However, activating a perfectly controllable physical behavior set reduces amygdala activity, optimizing adrenaline.
