## 1. The Principle of Serotonin Promotion by Nature Contact and Dirt

Humans have evolved to gain fundamental psychological stability when interacting with the soil. According to brain science and microbiological studies, when touching the dirt while gardening, we physically contact a beneficial microorganism called <strong>'Mycobacterium vaccae'</strong> living in the soil. This bacterium stimulates brain neurons through the skin and respiratory system to dramatically induce the release of <strong>serotonin</strong>, a neurotransmitter that helps emotional tranquility and alleviates depression. In other words, gardening provides both 'Attention Restoration', which relieves cognitive fatigue in the frontal lobe, and neuro-stabilizing effects.

## 3. Immerse Yourself Fully Only in the Fine Analog Textures of Dirt and Plants
