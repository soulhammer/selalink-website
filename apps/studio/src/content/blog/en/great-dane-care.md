Despite their imposing size, Great Danes are celebrated globally as 'Gentle Giants' due to their remarkably mild and loving nature. However, as a giant breed, they experience rapid skeletal growth that places heavy stress on their joints, and they face a dangerously high risk of Gastric Dilatation-Volvulus (GDV or Bloat), a life-threatening emergency. Precise, evidence-based care standards are therefore vital. Furthermore, given their physical strength, gentle and consistent socialization from puppyhood is imperative for safe coexistence with people and other animals. Today on PetSelf, we analyze the Great Dane's unique bonding signals and outline a 3-step routine for GDV prevention, joint integrity, and balanced temperament.
