## 1. Facial Feedback Hypothesis and Cognitive Benefits of Physical Tidiness

According to the Facial Feedback Hypothesis, the brain senses facial muscle contractions to determine emotional state. Simply making a smiling expression in the morning prompts the brain to recognize joy, encouraging the release of serotonin and dopamine while reducing cortisol.

Additionally, cleaning and organizing your surroundings blocks unnecessary visual noise that floods the visual cortex. The brain interprets visual clutter as a threat or unfinished task, inducing stress. When tidied, the brain's cognitive energy is restored, setting it up for immediate focus on core tasks.
