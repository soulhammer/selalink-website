The Maltipoo is a world-renowned designer hybrid dog combining the intelligence and low-shedding coat of the Toy Poodle with the affectionate, gentle nature of the Maltese. Adored for its teddy-bear appearance and playful charm, it ranks among the top choices for indoor companion dogs globally. However, as a small breed, Maltipoos are genetically predisposed to patellar luxation (slipping kneecaps), and their soft, wavy coat can easily form painful skin-level mats if not brushed daily. Additionally, their intense devotion to their human families makes them susceptible to separation anxiety, requiring early independence training. Today on PetSelf, we detail Maltipoo behavior signals, joint care strategies, and a 3-step daily routine.
