Have you ever bought a box of bright, sweet strawberries only to find the bottom ones mushy, bruised, and covered in fuzzy white mold a day or two later? 🍓

Strawberries are soft-hulled berries with extremely thin, delicate skins that make them highly sensitive to water absorption and mechanical pressure. Washing them beforehand, removing the calyx (green stem), or storing them in airtight containers creates the exact environment gray mold fungus (Botrytis) needs to thrive.

In this guide, shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on research from the USDA and RDA to completely prevent bruising, control moisture, and keep your strawberries fresh and sweet for over a week. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Sort out bruised or damaged strawberries immediately ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      A single bruised berry leaking juice releases fungal spores that spread through contact, spoiling the entire container within hours. Inspect your strawberries immediately upon purchase and eat bruised ones right away.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">

  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Store on deep inner shelves instead of the refrigerator door 🍓</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The refrigerator door shelf undergoes severe temperature swings and physical shocks every time it opens. Delicate berries should be kept on a deep inner shelf of the main compartment (0–2°C / 32–36°F) where temperature and movement remain stable.
    </p>
