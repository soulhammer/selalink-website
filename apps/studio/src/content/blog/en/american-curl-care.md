The American Curl is celebrated globally, especially in North America, for its gracefully backward-curling ears and highly affectionate, dog-like personality. Often referred to as the 'Peter Pan of cats' because they retain their kitten-like playfulness into adulthood, their unique ears are the result of a natural genetic mutation. The cartilage in their ears is firm, much like human ears, requiring gentle handling to prevent damage or breakage. Since their ears are harder to naturally clean, regular maintenance is vital. Furthermore, they are highly people-oriented and can suffer from stress if ignored. In this guide, we decode the American Curl's friendly body language and share a 3-step daily routine for ear health and emotional bonding.
