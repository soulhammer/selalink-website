Native to the tropical rainforests of Central and South America, the Green Iguana (Iguana iguana) is an iconic large diurnal lizard cherished by reptile enthusiasts worldwide. Growing up to 1.5 to 2 meters in length, these majestic reptiles possess strong territorial instincts and are exceptionally vulnerable to Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD) caused by inadequate Ultraviolet-B (UVB) radiation and calcium deficiencies. Furthermore, as strict herbivores, consuming animal protein induces fatal visceral gout and renal failure. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Green Iguana's body language and safeguard its health.
