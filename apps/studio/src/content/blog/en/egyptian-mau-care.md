The Egyptian Mau is the only naturally spotted breed of domesticated cat and a living treasure descended from the royal felines depicted in ancient Egyptian artwork. They are distinguished by striking 'gooseberry green' eyes, a scarab beetle marking on the forehead, and a unique flap of skin extending from the flank to the hind knee (flank flap). As the fastest domestic cat—capable of reaching speeds up to 30 mph (48 km/h)—providing 3D vertical territory and intense physical exercise is essential. Fiercely loyal and protective of their loved ones, they form deep bonds with their human family. In this guide, decodes Egyptian Mau body language and shares a 3-step daily care routine.
