Boasting brilliant yellow and orange plumage alongside an affectionate personality, the Sun Conure is a beloved medium-sized parrot worldwide. However, as highly intelligent flock animals, they have intense social needs, loud contact calls, and a tendency toward Vitamin A deficiency when fed seed-only diets. Furthermore, failing to provide 10 to 12 hours of quiet, dark sleep can cause stress-induced feather plucking and behavioral issues. In this guide, details a 3-step daily routine to protect your Sun Conure's health.
