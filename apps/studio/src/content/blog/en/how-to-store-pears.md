Have you ever bought juicy, crisp pears only to find them dry and mealy inside after storing them in the fridge? 🍐

Pears consist of over 85% water, making them highly susceptible to moisture loss and texture changes when exposed to ambient room air or dry environments. Furthermore, because pears are exceptionally sensitive to ethylene gas emitted by fruits like apples and bananas, improper storage leads to rapid softening and decay.

In this guide, introduces a scientifically proven 3-step pear storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to keep your pears crisp, sweet, and juicy for 1 to 2 months. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Wrap individually in newspaper or paper towels, then wrap with plastic wrap — Moisture Barrier 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">

The paper layer manages humidity while the plastic wrap prevents internal moisture evaporation, maintaining crispness for a long time.
  </p>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Store at low temperature (0–2°C) in the crisper drawer — Cold Storage (30–60 Days) ❄️</h4>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never store pears with ethylene-producing fruits like apples or bananas 🚫</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Pears are highly sensitive to ethylene gas. Storing them in the same compartment as high-ethylene emitters like apples, bananas, or tomatoes accelerates tissue breakdown, causing them to turn mushy and rot rapidly.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Do not wash pears before storing 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      The dry surface of the pear skin acts as a protective barrier against microorganisms. Washing them prior to storage traps residual moisture, creating an ideal environment for mold and bacteria to grow. Lightly wipe off dust with a dry cloth and wash right before eating.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
