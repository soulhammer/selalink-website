The Golden Retriever, beloved worldwide for its golden coat and gentle personality, is an intelligent and highly affectionate companion. While known as 'angelic,' they possess high athletic energy as former retrieving gundogs and require active daily physical and mental stimulation. Preventing genetically vulnerable hip dysplasia and satisfying their natural instinct to retrieve are critical for their lifelong health and emotional stability. Today, PetSelf explores Golden Retriever body language and introduces a 3-step healthy routine for joint care and interactive play.

## 1. Golden Retriever Body Language: Understanding Trust and Communication Signals

  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Golden Retrievers express emotions actively through facial expressions and body gestures. A key trust and calming signal is 'soft eye contact'. Unlike tense, adversarial staring, gently squinting while maintaining eye contact represents deep affection, trust, and a desire for psychological bonding. Responding with gentle verbal praise triggers oxytocin release, deepening their sense of security.
