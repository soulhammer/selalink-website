The Golden Retriever, beloved worldwide for its golden coat and gentle personality, is an intelligent and highly affectionate companion. While known as 'angelic,' they possess high athletic energy as former retrieving gundogs and require active daily physical and mental stimulation. Preventing genetically vulnerable hip dysplasia and satisfying their natural instinct to retrieve are critical for their lifelong health and emotional stability. In this guide, we explore Golden Retriever body language and introduces a 3-step healthy routine for joint care and interactive play.
