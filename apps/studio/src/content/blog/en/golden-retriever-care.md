The Golden Retriever, beloved for its golden coat and gentle personality, is an intelligent and friendly companion. While known as 'angelic,' they possess high energy as former retrieving gundogs and require active daily stimulation. Preventing genetically vulnerable hip dysplasia and satisfying their instinct to retrieve are critical for their health and happiness. Today, PetSelf explores Golden Retriever signals and introduces a 3-step healthy routine for joints and play.

## 1. Golden Retriever Body Language: Understanding Trust and Signals

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Golden Retriever Body Language: Understanding Trust and Signals</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Golden Retrievers express emotions actively through facial expressions and gestures. A key calming and trust signal is 'soft eye contact.' Unlike tense staring, squinting gently while maintaining eye contact represents deep affection, trust, and psychological bonding. Responding with gentle verbal praise triggers oxytocin release, deepening emotional stability. On the contrary, a stiff body posture with a high-held tail indicates tension and requires caution.
