Renowned as both the beginner's favorite and the expert's treasure, the Guppy (Poecilia reticulata) is a widely loved livebearer freshwater fish. However, guppies are sensitive to poor filtration, ammonia spikes, and pH shock, which easily trigger Fin Rot or Clamped Fin disease. As livebearers that give birth to free-swimming fry rather than laying eggs, fry protection and pregnancy care are also essential. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your guppy's physical signals and maintain pristine water parameters.
