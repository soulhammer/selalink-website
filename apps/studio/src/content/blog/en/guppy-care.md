Renowned as both the beginner's favorite and the expert's treasure, the Guppy (Poecilia reticulata) is a widely loved livebearer freshwater fish. However, guppies are sensitive to poor filtration, ammonia spikes, and pH shock, which easily trigger Fin Rot or Clamped Fin disease. As livebearers that give birth to free-swimming fry rather than laying eggs, fry protection and pregnancy care are also essential. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your guppy's physical signals and maintain pristine water parameters.

## 1. Guppy Body Language: Clamped Fins and Surface Gasping

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Guppy Body Language: Clamped Fins and Surface Gasping</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Guppies communicate health and water quality through fin posture and swimming position. 'Clamped Fins'—holding the tail and dorsal fin tightly against the body while staying still—signals severe stress from ammonia accumulation or thermal shock. Conversely, 'Surface Gasping'—repeatedly mouthing at the water surface—is an urgent warning of low dissolved oxygen or gill damage from ammonia poisoning.

## 2. Guppy's Body Language & Behavioral Signals

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">Body Signal 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Clamped Fins & Staying Still in Corners (Clamped Fins)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> An environmental stress warning caused by rising ammonia/nitrite levels, pH shock, or temperature fluctuations.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Immediately test water parameters for ammonia and perform a 20-30% partial water change with temperature-matched, dechlorinated water.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">

  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A signal of low dissolved oxygen levels, filter failure, or impaired gill function due to ammonia toxicity.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Turn on an air diffuser/bubbler to boost oxygen, check filter flow, and verify water temperature (24°C–26°C).
