The Turkish Van is an ancient natural landrace breed originating near Lake Van in Türkiye, famous for its distinct 'Van pattern' (chalk-white body with color restricted to the head and tail) and striking odd-eyes (heterochromia). Unlike most domestic cats, the Turkish Van possesses a unique, cashmere-like single coat that is naturally water-resistant, giving them a legendary affinity for swimming and water play. However, their muscular build and high intelligence require generous vertical spaces and interactive hunting play to prevent boredom. Additionally, odd-eyed individuals with blue eyes carry a genetic link to congenital sensorineural deafness, requiring dedicated hearing checks. Furthermore, thorough drying of the inner ear and coat after water encounters is crucial to prevent otitis externa and skin dermatitis. In this guide, we cover Turkish Van behavior signals, water safety, and a 3-step care routine.
