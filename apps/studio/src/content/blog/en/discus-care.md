Native to the warm, calm river basins of the Amazon River in South America, the Discus Fish (Symphysodon) is hailed worldwide as the 'King of the Aquarium' for its disc-shaped body and breathtaking color patterns. While magnificent, Discus are exceptionally sensitive freshwater cichlids requiring high water temperatures (28–30°C) and pristine, soft, slightly acidic water (pH 6.0–6.8). Water pollution or nitrate accumulation triggers 'body darkening,' while internal parasite infections cause fatal 'Hole-in-the-Head Disease' (Hexamita). Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Discus's body language and protect its health.

    <strong>• Response:</strong> Test nitrate levels immediately and perform a 20–30% water change using temperature-matched dechlorinated water.
  </p>

  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> Signals immunosuppression from low temperature or active Hexamita internal parasite infection in the digestive tract.<br/>
    <strong>• Response:</strong> Elevate water temperature to 30–31°C and consult an aquatic vet for metronidazole/Hexamita medication.
