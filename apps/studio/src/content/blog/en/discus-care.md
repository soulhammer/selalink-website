    answer: "Hole-in-the-Head disease is caused by the flagellate parasite Spironucleus/Hexamita, compounded by vitamin/mineral deficiencies and organic water pollution (high nitrates). The parasite impairs nutrient absorption in the gut, causing cartilage necrosis and sensory pit erosion on the head. Prevent this by siphoning leftover food daily, maintaining pristine water quality, and feeding vitamin-enriched foods."
  - question: "Why must Discus aquariums be kept at a high temperature of 28–30°C?"
    answer: "Discus originate from warm Amazonian backwaters where their metabolic and immune systems evolved to thrive in high heat. Temperatures below 26°C depress their metabolism, cause anorexia, and severely weaken immunity against Ich and internal flagellates. Maintaining a constant 28–30°C (or 30–31°C for juveniles) using reliable heaters is mandatory."

Native to the warm, calm river basins of the Amazon River in South America, the Discus Fish (Symphysodon) is hailed worldwide as the 'King of the Aquarium' for its disc-shaped body and breathtaking color patterns. While magnificent, Discus are exceptionally sensitive freshwater cichlids requiring high water temperatures (28–30°C) and pristine, soft, slightly acidic water (pH 6.0–6.8). Water pollution or nitrate accumulation triggers 'body darkening,' while internal parasite infections cause fatal 'Hole-in-the-Head Disease' (Hexamita). In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Discus's body language and protect its health.

    <summary class="flex justify-between items-center font-bold text-slate-900 dark:text-white list-none text-base md:text-lg tracking-tight" role="button" aria-expanded="true">
      <span class="pr-4">What causes 'Hole-in-the-Head Disease' (Hexamita) in Discus fish and how can it be prevented?</span>
      <span class="transition-transform group-open:rotate-180 text-xs text-slate-400 dark:text-slate-500 flex-shrink-0">▼</span>
    </summary>
    <p class="mt-3 text-sm md:text-base text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed pl-1 m-0 font-normal">
      Hole-in-the-Head disease is caused by the flagellate parasite Spironucleus/Hexamita, compounded by vitamin/mineral deficiencies and organic water pollution (high nitrates). The parasite impairs nutrient absorption in the gut, causing cartilage necrosis and sensory pit erosion on the head. Prevent this by siphoning leftover food daily, maintaining pristine water quality, and feeding vitamin-enriched foods.
    </p>
  </details>

    <summary class="flex justify-between items-center font-bold text-slate-900 dark:text-white list-none text-base md:text-lg tracking-tight" role="button" aria-expanded="false">
      <span class="pr-4">Why must Discus aquariums be kept at a high temperature of 28–30°C?</span>
      <span class="transition-transform group-open:rotate-180 text-xs text-slate-400 dark:text-slate-500 flex-shrink-0">▼</span>
    </summary>
    <p class="mt-3 text-sm md:text-base text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed pl-1 m-0 font-normal">
      Discus originate from warm Amazonian backwaters where their metabolic and immune systems evolved to thrive in high heat. Temperatures below 26°C depress their metabolism, cause anorexia, and severely weaken immunity against Ich and internal flagellates. Maintaining a constant 28–30°C (or 30–31°C for juveniles) using reliable heaters is mandatory.
    </p>
