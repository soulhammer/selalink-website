## 1. Attention Restoration & Motivational Mechanism of Mental Imagery

Experiencing solitude in great nature before a critical decision coordinates Directed Attention Fatigue and strongly exerts stress resilience. According to cognitive psychology, observing vast nature away from noise immediately restores the brain's attention resources. Furthermore, <strong>'Mental Imagery'</strong>, which draws a vivid and concrete picture of the goal achieved in the brain, warms up the motor cortex and visual cortex activated during actual movement. This derives subconscious action plans and drives cognitive thrust and willpower toward goals to their peak.

## 3. Cast Attention on Macroscopic Calmness Far Away, Not Near Noise

  <div>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      To reap the cognitive recovery effects of visualization meditation, you must broaden your depth of field. A brain that only looks at a desk or a narrow screen in front of it remains in a narrow focus state that encourages tension and anxiety. Just as Genghis Khan stared at the vast steppe and distant horizon from the mountain top, intentionally adopting a broad focus by staring at distant peaks, sky, or the horizon allows the brain to turn off threat responses and secure room to derive creative insights.
