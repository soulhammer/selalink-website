## 1. Physical Arousal and Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor (BDNF)

High-intensity workouts or active sports increase cardiovascular pumping, raising blood flow and oxygen levels in the prefrontal cortex immediately. Exercise stimulates the release of BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor), which promotes neurogenesis and synaptic plasticity. It also triggers dopamine and norepinephrine release, maximizing selective focus and problem-solving abilities for high-level cognitive tasks.
