Have you ever bought a mesh bag of onions and stacked them in a balcony corner, only to find them softening, rotting at contact points, and sprouting a few days later? 🧅

Onions are bulb vegetables that continue to respire and absorb/release humidity after harvest. When stored in humid, enclosed environments, Botrytis neck rot and soft rot bacteria proliferate rapidly. Furthermore, storing onions alongside potatoes triggers mutual moisture absorption and ethylene reactions, ruining both vegetables instantly.

In this guide, shares a scientifically proven 3-step storage guide based on research from the USDA and RDA to control moisture and gas, preserving crisp, firm onions for over a month. 🛡️

## 1. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Hang unpeeled whole onions in a cool, well-ventilated area — Room Temp Storage (30 Days) 🌬️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">

This prevents them from touching each other, eliminating pressure bruises and soft rot.
  </p>

    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">Dry peeled onions thoroughly and wrap individually in plastic wrap — Cold Storage (14–21 Days) 📦</h4>
  </div>

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Never seal unpeeled onions in plastic bags with trapped moisture 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Enclosing moisture-laden onions in plastic bags or airtight containers traps respiration humidity, causing skin softening and white mold growth within a single day. Always keep unpeeled whole onions in a dry, ventilated area.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Isolate bruised or soft onions immediately 🧅</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      An onion with cuts or rot emits dozens of times more decay gases and moisture than healthy ones, accelerating rotting in neighboring onions. Periodically check stored onions, trim away damaged spots, and use them immediately.
    </p>
  </div>
