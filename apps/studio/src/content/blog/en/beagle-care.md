With their cute floppy ears and happy white-tipped tail, Beagles are historic scent hounds. While friendly, they have high curiosity and a powerful drive to track scents. Without proper outlet, they can develop separation anxiety, leading to howling and destructive behavior. Additionally, their long floppy ears trap moisture, making them prone to yeast infections. Today, PetSelf decodes Beagle tracking and anxiety signals, and introduces a 3-step care routine.
