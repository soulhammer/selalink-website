Adored for their large floppy ears and happy white-tipped tail, Beagles are historic scent hounds that worked alongside humans for centuries. While famously friendly and adaptable, they possess an intense innate curiosity and a powerful drive to track scents. If this massive energy is not properly funneled, they easily develop separation anxiety, leading to vocal howling and destructive indoor habits. Furthermore, their long pendulous ears trap moisture and heat, creating a breeding ground for fungal ear infections (otitis externa). Today, PetSelf decodes Beagle tracking and anxiety signals and presents a 3-step daily care routine.
