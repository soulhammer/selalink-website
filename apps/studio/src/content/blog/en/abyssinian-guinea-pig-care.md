Abyssinian guinea pigs are widely loved for their unique rosettes (swirls of fur) and active, curious nature. Because they are rodents, their bodies cannot synthesize Vitamin C naturally. A lack of Vitamin C rapidly leads to scurvy, swollen joints, and low immunity. Additionally, they have very sensitive respiratory tracts, making cage hygiene and dust-free hay selection vital. Today, PetSelf decodes guinea pig vocal and physical signals and shares a 3-step homecare routine for Vitamin C enrichment and respiratory health.
