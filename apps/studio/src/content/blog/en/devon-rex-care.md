Often described as a 'pixie in a cat suit,' the Devon Rex is a beloved small-to-medium cat famed for its oversized ears, large expressive eyes, and soft, rippled wavy coat. Renowned for their dog-like devotion and habit of perching on their owners' shoulders, Devon Rexes are delightful companions. However, their delicate single coat makes them poorly insulated and highly sensitive to cold ambient temperatures. Furthermore, they are prone to oily skin secretions leading to Malassezia yeast dermatitis, and carry genetic risks for Devon Rex Myopathy (Spasticity) and Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM). In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Devon Rex's body language and safeguard its health.
