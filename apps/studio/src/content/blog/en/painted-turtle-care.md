Renowned for their striking red and yellow markings and smooth carapace, Painted Turtles (Chrysemys picta) are among the most beloved aquatic turtles worldwide. However, if their water quality deteriorates or they lack a proper basking dock to completely dry their shell, they easily develop 'Shell Rot' (bacterial/fungal ulcerative shell disease). Furthermore, inadequate UVB exposure and calcium intake cause 'Metabolic Bone Disease' (MBD), leading to soft, deformed shells. Unregulated room temperature drops below 20°C (68°F) can also suppress their immune system, causing fatal respiratory infections. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to understand your Painted Turtle's signals and protect its health.
