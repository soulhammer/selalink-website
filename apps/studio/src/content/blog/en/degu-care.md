Native to the Chilean Andes, Degus (Octodon degus) are close relatives of chinchillas and guinea pigs, celebrated for their high intelligence and vast vocal repertoire. Genetically, degus lack the metabolic capacity to process free sugars, making them extremely prone to Diabetes Mellitus and diabetic cataracts if fed fruits or sweet treats. Furthermore, their continuously growing incisors require constant grinding to prevent dental malocclusion, and their tails feature a 'degloving' defense mechanism that strips skin off if grabbed. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to read your Degu's signals and protect its health.
