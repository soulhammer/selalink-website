Have you ever bought a firm, crisp radish only to find it spongy, hollow, or wrinkled inside after a few days? 🧊

Radishes contain up to 94% water. When stored with their leafy tops attached, the greens continuously pull water and sugars from the root, causing the radish to turn spongy and hollow (pithy). Without proper moisture retention, the texture rapidly deteriorates.

Today, FreshSnap introduces a scientifically proven 3-step radish storage guide recommended by agriculture and food safety experts to maintain crispness and sweet flavor for up to 2 months. 🛡️

## 2. Essential Storage Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Cut off leafy tops/greens immediately after purchase ✂️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Radish greens continue to grow after harvest, rapidly siphoning moisture and nutrients from the root. Leaving the tops attached causes the radish to become pithy and spongy within days.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">Avoid freezing temperatures below 0°C (Prevent Freezing Injury) ❄️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      Because radishes consist mostly of water, temperatures below 0°C (32°F) rupture their cell walls, causing them to turn soft and mushy upon thawing. Always maintain cold temperatures at 0–3°C.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
