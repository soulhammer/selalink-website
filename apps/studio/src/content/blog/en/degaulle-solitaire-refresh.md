## 1. Brain Reset to Turn Off Decision Fatigue and Amygdala Overload

Making decisions continuously depletes prefrontal cortex energy, leading to <strong>decision fatigue</strong>. Under this state, cortisol spikes and the amygdala triggers hyper-reactive stress responses. Playing Solitaire, which has structured, non-threatening rules, transitions the brain from active judgment to passive pattern matching. Research indicates that this non-verbal immersion deactivates <strong>decision-making filters</strong> in the prefrontal cortex, allowing it to rest. Aligning shapes and numbers stabilizes functional connectivity between the prefrontal cortex and amygdala, rebuilding resilience.
