The Jindo dog is Korea's national treasure and a globally recognized breed known for its loyalty and intelligence. They share an intense bond with their primary handler, but due to their high alertness and one-owner nature, missing their socialization window can make them overly defensive toward strangers or other animals. 🐕 Additionally, they possess a dense double coat evolved for distinct seasonal climates, leading to heavy shedding during seasonal transitions. In this guide, we share socialization tips to reduce caution and a 3-step homecare routine to manage their double coat.
