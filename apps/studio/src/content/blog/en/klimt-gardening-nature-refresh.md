## 1. Mechanism: Natural Fractal Observation and Attention Restoration Theory (ART)

Working under artificial screens consumes prefrontal <strong></strong>, causing severe fatigue. According to <strong></strong>, natural organic fractal patterns induce 'Soft Fascination' without demanding effortful cognitive energy.

Observing natural flowers and morning light—like Klimt—instantly restores depleted prefrontal attention capacity and refreshes creative visual senses.
