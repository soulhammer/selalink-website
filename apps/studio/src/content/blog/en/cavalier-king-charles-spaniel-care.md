Cherished by British royalty for centuries, the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel is a world-renowned companion famed for its silky coat, large expressive eyes, and gentle, loving nature. As the ultimate 'lapdog,' they thrive on human companionship. However, they carry an exceptionally high genetic risk for Mitral Valve Disease (MVD) and are prone to Syringomyelia (SM) due to structural cranial space limitations. Furthermore, their long, pendulous ears require constant care to prevent ear infections. In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Cavalier's body language and protect their health.
