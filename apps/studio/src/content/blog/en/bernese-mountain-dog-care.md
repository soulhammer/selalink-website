Originating from the Swiss Alps, the Bernese Mountain Dog ('Berner') is a world-renowned large working breed cherished for its striking tricolor coat, sturdy physique, and placid, loving temperament. Highly devoted and gentle with families, Berners have a deep-chested anatomical structure that makes them extremely vulnerable to life-threatening Gastric Dilatation-Volvulus (GDV/Bloat) if exercised after eating. Furthermore, they carry an exceptionally high genetic predisposition to cancers, particularly Histiocytic Sarcoma, and face joint risks such as Hip and Elbow Dysplasia (HD/ED). In this guide, presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Berner's body language and protect its health.
