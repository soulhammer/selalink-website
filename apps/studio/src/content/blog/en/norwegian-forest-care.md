The Norwegian Forest Cat, affectionately called the 'Wegie', evolved to survive the harsh Scandinavian winters, developing a large, sturdy skeleton and a dense, water-repellent triple coat. As a large feline breed, they possess high active energy and a powerful natural instinct to climb high spaces. 🐈 However, their heavy coat requires consistent grooming to prevent severe hairballs and skin inflammation, while jumping from high towers can place strain on their joints. Today, PetSelf decodes the unique body language of the Norwegian Forest Cat and introduces a 3-step homecare routine to satisfy their vertical drive and protect their coat and joints.
