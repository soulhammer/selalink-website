## 1. Mechanism: Sensory Input Triggering and Speed-of-Capture Externalization

Sensory stimuli from fresh morning air activate visual and auditory cortices, unlocking associative connections. However, fleeting creative sparks degrade within 1-2 minutes if not recorded.

Vivaldi's rapid capture method externalizes raw ideas into physical format without hesitation or analytical pre-censorship, preserving 100% of intuitive sparks.
