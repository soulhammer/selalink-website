The Purple Pincher Hermit Crab (*Coenobita clypeatus*) is the most cherished land hermit crab species worldwide, recognized by its distinct purple large claw, round eyes, and lifelong need to swap shells as it grows. Though terrestrial, they breathe via modified gills, making habitat humidity (75–85%) critical to their survival. Additionally, they require a deep substrate—at least three times their height—of sand and coconut fiber to burrow safely during ecdysis (molting). Being social animals, they thrive in groups rather than solitary setups. Today, PetSelf breaks down hermit crab behaviors and outlines a 3-step daily care routine.
