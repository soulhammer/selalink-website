Renowned for their intelligence, elegant physique, and unwavering loyalty, the Doberman Pinscher is a cherished companion and working dog worldwide. While deeply devoted to their families, Dobermans are genetically predisposed to Dilated Cardiomyopathy (DCM) and, due to their deep-chested structure, are highly vulnerable to fatal Gastric Dilatation-Volvulus (GDV/Bloat). Furthermore, their single short coat makes them sensitive to cold, and without sufficient physical and mental stimulation, they can develop stress behaviors. Today, PetSelf presents a 3-step daily care routine to decode your Doberman's body signals and safeguard their health.
