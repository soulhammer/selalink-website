"No book is so bad that some good cannot be got from it." Pliny the Elder, the Roman administrator and author, believed that time not spent learning was wasted. During meals, baths, and travels, a slave read aloud to him while another took dictation. He never read a book without making extracts, establishing a continuous knowledge pipeline that generated the ancient world's most massive encyclopedia.

## 1. Why Excerpting Beats Passive Reading

When you read passively, information slips out of working memory quickly. When you physically write down extracts, you trigger the Reticular Activating System (RAS) through kinesthetic movement. Hand-writing requires your brain to process, filter, and summarize, which acts as a powerful retrieval cue and moves information from temporary working memory into long-term memory structures.
