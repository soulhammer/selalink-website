With a long flexible body and sparkling eyes, the Marshall Ferret is a highly social pet that interacts closely with humans. They require daily out-of-cage play to satisfy their curiosity and prevent depression. Regular ear checks are also essential to avoid mites. Today on PetSelf, we cover the ferret war dance, dooking vocalizations, and a 3-step homecare routine for their safe daily adventures.
