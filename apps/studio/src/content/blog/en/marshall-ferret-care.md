With a long, flexible torso and mischievous, sparkling eyes, the Marshall Ferret is an extraordinarily social carnivore that bonds deeply and plays enthusiastically with its human companions. However, retaining wild hunting instincts and boundless curiosity, ferrets require dedicated out-of-cage exploration time every day to burn off energy and prevent behavioral depression. Additionally, due to their ear canal anatomy, they are prone to ear mites and infections, making regular hygiene checks essential. When overcome with joy, ferrets perform a chaotic, bouncing dance by twisting their bodies sideways. Today, PetSelf decodes the ferret war dance and dooking vocalizations while introducing a 3-step homecare routine for safe daily adventures.
