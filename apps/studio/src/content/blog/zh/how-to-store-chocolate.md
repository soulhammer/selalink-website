巧克力中的核心成分可可脂对温度和湿度变化极度敏感，且会像海绵一样迅速吸收周围的挥发性异味。如果直接裸露放入冰箱，水汽凝结会导致糖分溶解重结晶产生“起霜（Sugar Bloom）”，或可可脂融化重结晶产生“脂霜（Fat Bloom）”，严重破坏其丝滑口感与浓郁香气。

## 1. 科学验证的储存指南

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">用铝箔纸或密封盒双层包裹 — 阻绝异味与湿气 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    保存巧克力的关键在于彻底隔绝空气与异味。💡储存小贴士：拆封后的巧克力先用铝箔纸或保鲜膜包裹紧实，再装入密封拉链袋或保鲜盒中进行双层隔离。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">存放在15 ~ 18℃阴凉干燥处 — 最佳常温保存 (最长1年) 🍫</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    巧克力保持最佳品质的适宜温度为15 ~ 18℃，湿度需低于55%。💡储存小贴士：除炎热夏季外，建议存放在温度稳定的酒柜、阴凉橱柜或抽屉中，优于直接放冰箱。
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-gradient-to-b from-emerald-500/[0.04] to-transparent dark:border-emerald-500/20 dark:bg-emerald-950/20 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md transition-all">
  <div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs font-black tracking-wider bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-700 dark:bg-emerald-500/20 dark:text-emerald-300 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/30 shadow-xs">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-lg md:text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-slate-100 m-0">夏季/长期保存需密封冷藏并分步解冻 — 冷藏·冷冻保存 ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0 font-normal">
    室温高于20℃时，必须密封后冷藏或冷冻。💡储存小贴士：连同密封盒放入冷藏室或冷冻室。取出食用时，切勿立即开盖，需连盒在常温下静置30分钟以上完全回温，以防凝结水珠。
  </p>
</div>

## 2. 储存必需规则及注意事项

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">切勿未密封直接放入冰箱冷藏 ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      冰箱内部的高湿度会导致巧克力表面凝结水珠，溶解糖分并在干燥后形成白色粉末状的“起糖霜”。此外，可可脂会极易吸收大蒜、洋葱等强烈异味。
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 md:p-6 rounded-r-2xl rounded-l-md border-l-4 border-amber-500/30 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 bg-amber-500/[0.06] dark:bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30 flex items-start gap-4 shadow-xs backdrop-blur-xs">
  <span class="text-xl md:text-2xl flex-shrink-0 mt-0.5">⚠️</span>
  <div class="flex-1 min-w-0">
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block text-base md:text-lg mb-1.5 tracking-tight">避开阳光直射及20℃以上的高温 ☀️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 text-sm md:text-base m-0 leading-relaxed font-normal break-words">
      环境温度超过20℃时，可可脂就会开始融化并上浮，引发“起脂霜”现象，导致巧克力质地变渣、失去光泽和风味。
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
