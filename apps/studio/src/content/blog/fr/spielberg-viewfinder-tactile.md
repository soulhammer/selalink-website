## 1. Stimulation du cortex somatosensoriel et accélération du lobe parietal

Former un cadre physique active le traitement spatial dans le lobe pariétal et aide le cortex visuel à ignorer le bruit périphérique, facilitant l'attention sélective.

## 2. Routine Pratique en 3 Étapes pour l’Homme Moderne

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20 tracking-wider">ÉTAPE 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Choix d'un objet tactile 3D analogique</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Place an analog object with distinct physical shapes, such as a miniature model car, a director's viewfinder, or a wooden cube, on your desk. This prepares you for controlled sensory activation.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20 tracking-wider">ÉTAPE 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Exploration tactile de 10 minutes et réduction visuelle</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    When a cognitive block occurs, feel the edges and surfaces of the model with your fingertips for tactile input. Simultaneously, narrow your vision using a small card hole or lens, and stare at a single object for 1 minute to filter out surrounding visual noise.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20 tracking-wider">ÉTAPE 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Retour à la conception structurelle spatiale</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Immediately after haptic stimulation, restart your process layout or programming design using the spatial reasoning of your activated right parietal lobe. Your brain will sketch structures more intuitively after receiving tactile feedback.
  </p>
