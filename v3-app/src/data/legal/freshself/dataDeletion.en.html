! FreshSelf Data Policy (Data Deletion & Purging Instructions)

The FreshSelf Application respects your privacy and operates in a 100% local, offline mode. Any registered ingredient records, spaces, and photos you enter are stored exclusively on your local device and are never sent to or stored on our remote servers. Therefore, manual data deletion requests via email or our website are unnecessary as there is no data on our servers to delete.

Method A: In-App Complete Data Reset

Within the Application, navigate to the [Settings] tab and trigger 'Reset App Data'. This will instantly hard-delete all SQLite database records and local WEBP image sandbox files from your device storage.

Method B: Direct Application Deletion (Uninstall)

Simply uninstall the Application from your mobile device. Per standard mobile OS protocols, the Application sandbox directory along with all local databases and cached photos will be immediately and permanently removed.

Local Purged Data Categories

  • Local SQLite database tables representing Spaces information
  • Local SQLite database tables representing registered Items
  • Ingredient consumption history and logs
  • All WEBP photo assets stored in the Application's local sandbox

No Remote Data Storage Policy

As the Application is completely self-contained, no identifying profiles or inventory records are uploaded to our databases. If you have any inquiries regarding this policy, please reach out to us at support@selalink.net.
