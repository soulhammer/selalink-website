## 2. Scientifically proven 3-step storage guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">[PT] Wipe Off Surface Oil and Moisture — Prevent Spoilage 🍣</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    [PT] Surface moisture and fish oil oxidise quickly when touching air, creating fishy smells. Wipe the salmon dry using paper towels.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">[PT] Apply Lemon Juice or Sake — Natural Acid Preservation 🍋</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    [PT] Lightly coat the salmon surface with sake or lemon juice. This acid/alcohol coat limits bacterial growth and lipid oxidation.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">[PT] Wrap Tightly with Cling Film — Airtight Storage 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    [PT] Place the salmon on cling wrap, wrap it tightly to expel air, and store in the coldest part of the fridge. Consume within 2 days.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial rules to avoid

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 dark:bg-rose-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">[PT] Inspect color changes carefully ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      [PT] If the salmon turns dull brown or lacks elasticity when pressed, the proteins have broken down. Discard it immediately.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">[PT] Remove skin and bones for freezing ❄️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      [PT] For long-term freezing (up to 60 days), removing the skin and bones beforehand preserves texture and makes thawing convenient.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
