## 2. Scientifically proven 3-step storage guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Conserve em Temperatura Ambiente Antes de Madurar 🌿</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Mantenha os tomates verdes ou firmes em área ventilada em temperatura ambiente (15-20 °C). Isso permite que madurem naturalmente.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Remova os Pedúnculos Verdes — Evitar Bolor ✂️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Os pedúnculos verdes são vulneráveis à umidade e acumulam fungos. Removê-los antes de guardar prolonga a vida útil.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Embrulhe Tomates Maduros e Guarde na Gaveta de Legumes ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Assim que os tomates estiverem vermelhos e macios, embrulhe-os individualmente e coloque-os na gaveta de legumes. Consuma em 5-7 dias.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial rules to avoid

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 dark:bg-rose-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Não congele tomates crus 🍅</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      O frio interrompe as reações celulares. Os tomates verdes guardados na geladeira não amadurecem e estragam ao sair.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Cuidado com os emissores de etileno 🍎</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Os tomates são sensíveis aos gases de maturação. Guardá-los ao lado de maçãs ou bananas vai amaciá-los em um dia.
    </p>
