Guardar cebolas e batatas juntas na mesma cesta é um erro comum. Elas trocam gases e umidade acelerando o apodrecimento.

---

## 1. Why store like this? Scientific facts

Understanding the nature of each ingredient and following the proper storage method is extremely important.

---

## 2. Scientifically proven 3-step storage guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Armazenar com Casca em Rede Ventilada — Fluxo de Ar 🌬️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    As cebolas inteiras precisam de boa circulação de ar para se manterem secas. Coloque-as em uma bolsa de rede em uma despensa escura.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Seque as Cebolas Descascadas e Embrulhe com Filme — Selar na Geladeira 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    As cebolas descascadas ou cortadas devem ser secas completamente. Limpe a umidade da superfície e embrulhe firmemente com filme plástico.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Isolar Completamente das Batatas — Evitar Apodrecimento Mútuo 🛡️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    As cebolas liberam etileno, fazendo brotar as batatas, enquanto as batatas emitem umidade que amolece as cebolas. Guarde-as separadamente.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial rules to avoid

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 dark:bg-rose-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>

    </p>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">

  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Isole imediatamente cebolas danificadas 🧅</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Uma única cebola podre libera muita umidade e infecta as vizinhas. Verifique sua despensa regularmente e separe as macias.
