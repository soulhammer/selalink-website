## 2. Scientifically proven 3-step storage guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">[FR] Store Room Temp in Paper Bag Before Ripe — Natural Ripening 🥑</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    [FR] Keep firm avocados at 15-20°C. Placing them inside a paper bag with an apple or banana accelerates ripening via natural ethylene gas.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">[FR] Wrap with Plastic Cling Film and Refrigerate Once Ripe — Delay Decay ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    [FR] Once the skin turns dark brown and yields to gentle pressure, wrap it tightly in plastic cling wrap and keep in the crisper drawer to prolong freshness.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">[FR] Apply Lemon Juice to Cut Surfaces and Seal — Prevent Oxidation 🍋</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    [FR] Keep the pit inside the unused half, apply a thin layer of lemon juice or olive oil on the cut surface, and wrap tightly to block oxygen and browning.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial rules to avoid

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 dark:bg-rose-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">[FR] Never refrigerate unripe avocados ⚠️</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      [FR] Keeping hard green avocados in the fridge causes cold injury, preventing them from ripening permanently even when brought back to room temperature.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">

    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      [FR] Large black spots on the skin combined with extreme mushiness indicate internal decay. Avoid consumption.
