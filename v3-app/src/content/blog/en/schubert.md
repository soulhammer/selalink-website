Franz Schubert, a genius Austrian composer representing 19th century Romantic music and called the King of Lieder (Liederfürst), composed more than 600 great and lyrical songs and symphonies until his young death at the young age of 31. As soon as he opened his eyes every morning, without taking off his glasses, he went straight to the composition desk right next to his bedside and began writing down notes on music paper. In order not to lose sight of the musical unconsciousness that arose even in his half-asleep state, he went to bed without his glasses on. This immediate writing routine, which eliminated all the friction between waking up in the morning and starting work, was what made him an immortal composer.

Today on BuildSelf, we introduce the cognitive scientific principles of the 'immediate writing routine that completely eliminates the friction between waking up and starting work' that he adheres to, and a 3-step guide that modern people can easily practice in their daily lives.

## 1. Analysis of brain science principles that lead to success

Immediately after waking up, the human brain maintains a state of creative Theta waves, before logical cognitive filters and distracting thoughts take over. If, like Schubert, you completely block the preparatory steps between waking up and key creative actions (checking your smartphone, washing your face, tidying your desk, etc.) and make the friction between your actions zero, you can fully lift up the brilliant intuitive ideas latent in the unconsciousness of your brain with your fingertips and pin them on paper.
