## 1. Physiological Mechanisms of Circadian Alignment and Motor Activation Through Dawn Workouts

Executing active physical workouts at the exact same early dawn hour daily aligns our internal biological clock. First, the brain's suprachiasmatic nucleus is programmed to raise body temperature and initiate cortisol secretion at that set hour, neutralizing the motor function drop typical after waking up. Second, consistent dawn exercise directs the autonomic nervous system to allocate metabolic energy early, rendering physical responsiveness and cardiorespiratory efficiency as sharp as daytime.
