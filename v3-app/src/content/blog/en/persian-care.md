With their thick coat and flat face, Persian cats are known for their gentle and calm nature. However, their flat facial structure causes excessive tearing, which can lead to bacterial infections in their skin folds. Their dense longhair coat easily tangles, causing pain and stopping air circulation. Today, PetSelf decodes Persian cat eye and coat stress signals and provides a 3-step homecare routine.

## 1. Persian Cat Body Language: Silent Signs from Eyes and Ears

Because Persians express emotions quietly, detecting stress can be difficult. While they show affection through 'half-closed eyes' (blinking slowly), 'airplane ears' (ears turned sideways) signal discomfort or annoyance. This is commonly observed when brushing is done too quickly or their face is touched roughly.

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">SIGNAL 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Half-Closed Eyes</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> A peaceful message indicating deep trust in the owner and a highly satisfied, comfortable state.<br/>
    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> Respond by blinking slowly at them in return and talking to them in a calm, soothing voice.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">

    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Airplane Ears</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> An expression of discomfort caused by fur pulling during brushing or tension from surrounding noise.<br/>
    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> Immediately stop brushing or touching and give the cat space to calm down in a quiet spot.
  </p>

## 2. Custom Daily Care Routine

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Morning: Facial Fold Cleaning and Tear Stain Management</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    In the morning, gently clean their facial folds and under-eye area with warm saline or a veterinary tear wipe to prevent red yeast dermatitis.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Afternoon: Dual Brushing with Slicker and Steel Comb</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Focus on high-friction areas like armpits and belly. Use a slicker brush first to detangle undercoat knots, then use a steel comb to align and check skin aeration.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Evening: Hairball Remedy and Water Fountain Check</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Give hairball paste to prevent intestinal blockage from swallowed hair, and inspect their water fountain to encourage proper hydration.
  </p>
</div>
