Maltese, with their white, silky coat and three black dots (eyes and nose), are beloved companion dogs worldwide. While lively and affectionate, they express their boundaries clearly, earning them a spunky reputation. 🐕

However, Maltese owners often worry about two genetic issues: Patellar Luxation (dislocated kneecaps) and Tear Staining, which causes red streaks and potential skin infections. Daily lifestyle adjustments are crucial for managing these conditions.

Today, PetSelf explores Maltese body language and introduces a safe, 3-step daily care routine to protect their joints and eyes.

## 1. Maltese Body Language: Signals of Stress and Comfort

As dogs with high dependency on their owners, Maltese silently show their psychological state through body gestures. Typical calming signals include 'nose licking' and 'yawning'. When facing a tense or unfamiliar environment, licking their nose or yawning is not due to sleepiness; it is a peaceful message to calm themselves down and ask others not to threaten them. If owners continue excessive contact during this time, their stress increases. It is best to take a step back and give them space.

    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> Not due to sleepiness; it is a 'please calm down' signal to soothe themselves and others.<br/>
    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> If they yawn during training or grooming, stop immediately and provide a quiet space.

    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> If shown when held or approached by strangers, stop the physical contact and give them space.

## 2. Custom Daily Care Routine

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Morning: Slip Prevention Check and Joint Stretching</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Check that pet mats are secure. When your Maltese stretches in the morning, gently massage their hind leg joints to loosen stiff muscles. Ensure paw pad fur is trimmed.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Afternoon: 1:1 Eye Hygiene and Combing Routine</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Once a day, comb eye areas with fine-tooth combs and cleaning solutions. Wet fur leads to red stains and infections, so wipe moisture with cotton pads to keep dry.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Evening: Joint Supplement and Paw Desensitization</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Administer joint supplements. Touch their paws gently and immediately reward them with treats. This desensitization prevents aggressive reactions during claw clipping or physical checks.
  </p>
</div>
