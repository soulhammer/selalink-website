René Descartes, the father of modern philosophy who established the starting point of modern philosophy with the proposition "I think, therefore I am" and founded analytic geometry. Did you know that this giant of intellect maintained a very cozy yet unique routine every morning? 🛏️

Descartes was very weak since childhood, so it was very difficult for him to get up early in the morning. Fortunately, the principal of the royal school he attended specially allowed him to think in bed late in the morning. This habit continued throughout his life, even after he became an adult and volunteered for the army. He did not get out of bed until noon every day, lying comfortably to think, read, and write. He firmly believed that only when the body was completely lying down and relaxed could the mind operate most creatively and transparently without being disturbed by physical fatigue.

Today, BuildSelf introduces the scientific basis of Descartes' 'bed thinking' meditation routine and how busy modern people can use it in the morning.

## 1. 5 Minutes of Lying in the Morning Determines the Stability of the Day

Many modern people get up in a hurry when they hear the alarm, their hearts beating, check emails or messages on their phones, and start the day. This induces cortisol and adrenaline secretion from the start of the day, raising the arousal level excessively and making them feel rushed and chronically fatigued throughout the day.

On the other hand, maintaining a horizontal, comfortable lying posture for a while immediately after waking up like Descartes moderately activates the parasympathetic nervous system, helping the brain wake up gently. In this state, the **'Alpha wave'**, a brainwave that helps manifest high creativity and intuition, is harmoniously released. This brief morning lying meditation time firmly holds the cognitive function and emotional resilience of the entire day.

## 2. 5-Minute Bed Meditation of Descartes for Modern People

In a busy daily life that starts as soon as you open your eyes, we propose a modern Descartes routine that gives your brain a break and brings out the best mental condition.

  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Do not get out of bed immediately after turning off the morning alarm, and do not turn on the smartphone screen to search the internet or check notifications. Keep your body completely lying comfortably under the blanket, and keep the smartphone far away.
  </p>

  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Close your eyes comfortably or look at the ceiling, inhale deeply through your nose and exhale slowly through your mouth 5 times. Calmly observe the cooling of your head when inhaling, and the tension releasing from your shoulders, neck, and back when exhaling.
  </p>
