## 1. Neurophysiology of Dawn Light and Morning Movement Waking the Brain

The act of facing sunlight outdoors and walking after waking up at a set time completely stops the brain's <strong>melatonin</strong> secretion, normalizing serotonin and cortisol. The emotional stability from morning family conversations lowers anxiety and accelerates brain rotation, setting up the best cognitive infrastructure.
