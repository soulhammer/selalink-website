Edgar Allan Poe, a giant who is revered as a pioneer of strange and cold gothic horror literature and the founder of detective fiction. On what kind of tools were his genius manuscripts, which created the psychological anxiety of the human inside and perfectly structured logical mysteries, born? 📜

Edgar Allan Poe rarely used general rectangular sheets of paper when writing in his study. He wrote texts densely on narrow, long paper ribbons in extremely fine and neat handwriting. When one strip of paper was filled, he applied glue or wax to the end to paste the next strip of paper continuously. As the writing progressed, his manuscript became a long 'scroll' that was rolled up. Poe once confessed that when concentrating on this strange, narrow space of visual information, all senses of his brain converged to a single flow with deep focus.

Today, BuildSelf learns the visual feedback tuning principles hidden in Edgar Allan Poe's 'scroll writing' routine, and how to use modern focus modes to improve writing efficiency.

## 1. Cognitive Tunneling and Inhibition of Self-Monitoring Circuits

Controlling the visual width in an extremely narrow band shape reduces the brain's unnecessary eye tracking (saccadic movement), dramatically blocking the waste of visual attention in the frontal lobe. Brain science calls this state 'cognitive tunneling', which aids concentration by forcibly limiting the input width of visual information.

At the same time, the physical scroll structure that endlessly leads downwards inhibits a destructive habit common to writers: 'rereading and correcting the sentence just written.' When the brain's 'self-monitoring' circuit that evaluates sentences immediately is cut off, the brain can maintain 'free association', the organic flow of thoughts in the unrefined subconscious mind, until the end, enabling creative and fluent writing.

## 3. Isolation from Self-Editing
<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-indigo-500 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">💡</span>
  <div>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Correcting sentence by sentence while writing causes the brain's creative right hemisphere and critical left hemisphere to constantly clash, causing severe brain fatigue. Just as Poe wrote scrolls continuously downwards, the rule is to force turn off the critical editing circuit during writing, use brain energy only for pouring out ideas, and execute correction completely later.
    </p>
