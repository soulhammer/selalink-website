## 2. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Do not wash raw poultry with water — Prevent cross-contamination ❌💧</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Washing raw chicken splashes water containing Campylobacter or Salmonella bacteria up to 50cm around the sink, contaminating dishes, utensils, and countertops. Do not wash raw poultry; instead, pat it dry with paper towels or blanch it directly in boiling water.
  </p>

  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Seal in a double bag and store on the bottom shelf — Refrigerated Storage (2Days) 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    The raw juices leaking from poultry contain bacteria and can easily spoil other foods. Place the chicken in a plastic bag, seal it inside a second ziploc bag, and store it on the bottom shelf of the refrigerator where temperatures are coldest and most stable.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Use separate cutting boards and knives for raw chicken</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      The tools used for raw chicken must be thoroughly sanitized or washed. Using them directly on salads causes cross-contamination.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Consume within 2 days or freeze immediately</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Chicken has higher moisture and bacterial density than pork or beef, causing it to spoil very rapidly. Limit fridge storage to 48 hours.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
