Napoleon Bonaparte, the legendary military strategist and Emperor of France. Renowned for his endless energy, he managed state affairs and battles on just 4 hours of night sleep. His secret lay in 'micro-naps'—taking 10 to 15 minutes of sleep whenever he had a spare moment, even during battle. Today, BuildSelf reveals how short strategic naps can rapidly reset your cognitive performance.

## 1. Micro-Napping and Brain Wave Optimization

A 10 to 15-minute nap restricts sleep to NREM (Non-Rapid Eye Movement) Stage 1 and Stage 2. This prevents the brain from entering deep sleep, eliminating 'sleep inertia'—the groggy feeling upon waking. Instead, it clears adenosine (the chemical that causes sleepiness) from the brain, restoring attention, processing speed, and memory. In sleep science, these short power naps are proven to reset cognitive decline, returning performance to baseline in minutes.
