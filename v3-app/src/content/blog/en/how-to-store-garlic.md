## 2. Scientifically Proven Storage Guide

## 3. Crucial Rules and Cautions

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Isolate bruised or sprouted garlic cloves</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Bruised or sprouted garlic releases high moisture and decay signals, accelerating the rot of surrounding healthy cloves.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Do not underestimate mold on spoiled garlic</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      If blue or black mold appears, do not just wash it off and cook. Mold spores spread deeply into the flesh, making it unsafe.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
