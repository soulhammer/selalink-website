Abyssinian guinea pigs are widely loved for their unique rosettes (swirls of fur) and active, curious nature. Because they are rodents, their bodies cannot synthesize Vitamin C naturally. A lack of Vitamin C rapidly leads to scurvy, swollen joints, and low immunity. Additionally, they have very sensitive respiratory tracts, making cage hygiene and dust-free hay selection vital. Today, PetSelf decodes guinea pig vocal and physical signals and shares a 3-step homecare routine for Vitamin C enrichment and respiratory health.

## 1. Guinea Pig Communication: Wheeking and Popcorning

Guinea pigs are highly vocal and active animals that express their emotions clearly. They make a loud, high-pitched squeak called 'wheeking' when demanding food or greeting their owner. Conversely, they perform a sudden, jerky jump called 'popcorning' when they are extremely happy and excited.

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">SIGNAL 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Wheeking (Loud Squeaking)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> An excitement call or request, typically indicating they hear food packages opening or are asking for fresh vegetables.<br/>
    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> Provide fresh vegetables or Vitamin C treats, and use this opportunity to build a strong bond.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/5 dark:bg-emerald-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20">SIGNAL 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Popcorning (Sudden Jumping)</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    <strong>• Meaning:</strong> Jumping straight up in the air, sometimes twisting, showing pure joy, safety, and high spirits.<br/>
    <strong>• Correct Action:</strong> Ensure their play area is free of sharp corners or hazards so they can play safely.
  </p>
</div>

## 2. Custom Daily Care Routine

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Morning: Vitamin C Supplementation & Fresh Hay Check</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Administer vet-approved Vitamin C supplements directly (avoid putting it in water as it degrades). Fill the rack with fresh Timothy hay.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Afternoon: Cage Spot Cleaning & Dust Control</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Spot-clean soiled bedding areas. Shake Timothy hay outside before placing it in the cage to reduce respiratory dust.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Evening: Health Inspection & Social Interaction</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Check their nose and eyes for discharge (early signs of URI) and feel their rosettes for any skin issues. Spend time socializing.
  </p>
</div>
