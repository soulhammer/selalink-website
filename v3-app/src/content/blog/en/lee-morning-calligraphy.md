## 1. Brain Science Relationship Between Fine Motor Control in Calligraphy and Prefrontal Stability

Unlike stiff metal pens, calligraphy uses flexible brush tips, requiring extremely delicate adjustment of stroke thickness, speed, and force. This process highly engages the brain's primary motor cortex, physically blocking the entry of stray thoughts. Furthermore, the multi-sensory tactile, visual, and auditory feedback from grinding ink and hearing the brush on paper synchronizes association areas in the prefrontal cortex and cerebellum regulating the autonomic nervous system, lowering heart rate and inducing rational calmness.
