## 2. Scientifically proven 3-step storage guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Always Store Room Temperature Before Ripe — Natural Ripening 🌿</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Keep unripe or firm tomatoes in a well-ventilated area at room temperature (15-20°C). This allows natural lycopene and sugars to mature, creating juicy and rich fruits.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Remove Green Stems Before Storing — Prevent Mold ✂️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    The green stems are highly vulnerable to humidity and act as breeding grounds for mold. Removing stems before storage prevents bacterial attack and extends their shelf life significantly.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Wrap Fully Ripe Tomatoes and Keep in Crisper Drawer — Delay Decay ❄️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Once tomatoes are deep red and soft, wrap them individually in plastic wrap or paper, and place them in the refrigerator crisper drawer. Consume within 5-7 days.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial rules to avoid

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 dark:bg-rose-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Do not freeze raw tomatoes 🍅</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Cold temperatures stop cellular reactions permanently. Unripe tomatoes stored in the fridge will never mature and will rot instantly once brought to room temperature.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Beware of heavy ethylene emitters 🍎</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Tomatoes are moderately sensitive to ripening gases. Keeping them directly next to apples or bananas will turn them mushy within a single day.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
