Scottish Folds capture hearts worldwide with their folded ears, round eyes, and owl-like appearance. They possess a sweet, quiet nature and enjoy peaceful companionship by their owner's side. 🐈

However, the gene responsible for their folded ears also carries the risk of a severe genetic condition called Osteochondrodysplasia (hereditary cartilage disease). This causes their joint cartilage to stiffen, leading to chronic pain. Additionally, because their ears are folded, air circulation inside is restricted, making them highly susceptible to ear mites and infections.

Today, PetSelf decodes the unique physical signals of Scottish Folds experiencing joint pain and outlines a 3-step home care routine for joint protection and ear hygiene.

## 1. Fold's Body Language: Buddha Position and Tucking the Tail

Scottish Folds use unique postures to manage genetic joint and bone pain. Key indicators of discomfort include the 'Buddha position' and 'tail lowering'. Sitting upright with their legs stretched out (Buddha position) is not a cute trick; it is a desperate resting posture to relieve weight pressure from their spine and legs. Lowering their tail or tucking it between their hind legs warns that their tail joints are stiffening or painful. Avoid touching their lower spine or tail when you see this.

## 2. Custom Daily Care Routine

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Morning: Gait Analysis and Cartilage Support Supplements</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Watch their gait for 30 seconds when they wake up to check for limping. Administer joint supplements (glucosamine, MSM) with their morning meal.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Afternoon: Ear Canal Inspection and Weekly Aeration Cleaning</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Folded ears trap humidity. Do not use cotton swabs. Apply a few drops of feline ear cleaner, massage gently, and wipe away debris at the ear entrance with cotton pads.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-indigo-500/20 bg-indigo-500/5 dark:bg-indigo-500/10 shadow-sm">

    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-indigo-500/10 text-indigo-600 dark:text-indigo-400 rounded-full border border-indigo-500/20">ROUTINE 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Evening: Impact-absorbing Floor Padding and Controlled Low-impact Play</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Jumping down causes severe cartilage pain. Place impact-absorbing mats near furniture, and play low-intensity ground games that don't involve jumping or sharp twisting.
  </p>
