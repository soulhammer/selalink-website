## 1. Extreme Focus Offered by Uncompromising Success Experience

When the brain performs a task requiring high concentration, the most important catalyst is <strong>dopamine</strong>, a reward prediction substance. The experience of successfully finishing a task under a strict rule of returning to square one upon failure intensely activates the brain's pleasure and behavioral control centers. This process temporarily blocks all distractions and forces an optimal state of focus.
