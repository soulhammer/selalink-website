## 2. Scientifically proven 3-step storage guide

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 1</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Store with Peel in a Well-Ventilated Net — Air Flow 🌬️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Whole unpeeled onions need good air circulation to stay dry. Place them in a mesh bag or hanging net in a dark pantry, ensuring they don't pile up or touch each other.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 2</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Dry Peeled Onions and Wrap with Plastic Film — Fridge Seal 📦</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Peeled or cut onions must be dried completely. Wipe off surface moisture with a towel and wrap each tightly with plastic cling wrap to prevent oxidation and rot in the crisper drawer.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="my-8 p-6 md:p-8 rounded-[2rem] border border-slate-200/80 bg-white/50 dark:border-white/5 dark:bg-slate-900/30 shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-3 mb-4">
    <span class="px-3 py-1 text-xs font-bold bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 rounded-full border border-emerald-500/20 tracking-wider">STEP 3</span>
    <h4 class="text-xl font-extrabold text-slate-900 dark:text-white m-0">Isolate Completely from Potatoes — Mutual Decay Defense 🛡️</h4>
  </div>
  <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 leading-relaxed text-sm md:text-base m-0">
    Onions release ethylene gas which triggers potato sprouting, while potatoes emit moisture that makes onions soft and moldy. Keep them in completely separate spaces.
  </p>
</div>

## 3. Crucial rules to avoid

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-rose-500 bg-rose-500/5 dark:bg-rose-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Never seal wet onions at room temperature 💧</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Storing damp onions in an airtight container at room temperature traps humidity, leading to white mold and mushy onions within a day.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="my-6 p-5 rounded-2xl border-l-4 border-amber-500 bg-amber-500/5 dark:bg-amber-500/10 flex items-start gap-4">
  <span class="text-xl">⚠️</span>
  <div>
    <strong class="text-slate-950 dark:text-white font-extrabold block mb-1">Isolate damaged onions immediately 🧅</strong>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      A single rotting onion releases massive moisture and microbes, infecting neighbor onions. Inspect your pantry regularly and separate soft ones.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
