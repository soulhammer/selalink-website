Mother Teresa, who dedicated her life to helping the poor in Calcutta. How did she endure the mental limits and sorrow of the slums to maintain her emotional peace? 🕊️

Every day, Mother Teresa faced severe emotional exhaustion from interacting with hundreds of patients. To heal this, she woke up at 4:30 AM to practice silent contemplation before starting her day. Spending the first hour in absolute silence, she observed her thoughts and found inner peace. She wrote that this quiet time was the shield that washed away the emotional stains of yesterday.

Today, BuildSelf looks at the brain science benefits of Mother Teresa's 'morning silence' routine and a 15-minute guide for modern life.

## 1. Calibrating the Empathy Network and Inhibiting Amygdala Activation

Early morning silent contemplation stabilizes the hypothalamus-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis, suppressing sympathetic hyperarousal and regulating cortisol. This physically inhibits overactivation of the amygdala. Concurrently, it strengthens the functional connectivity between the prefrontal cortex and emotional centers, calibrating the empathy network to maintain peace without being overwhelmed by others' pain.

## 3. Errors in Suppressing Thoughts and Floating Tips

  <div>
    <p class="text-slate-700 dark:text-slate-300 text-sm m-0 leading-relaxed">
      Trying to force your brain to 'not think' (thought suppression) during silence triggers a rebound effect, making distracting thoughts spin even faster. When thoughts arise, do not fight them. Simply observe them pass like floating clouds, and redirect your focus to the airflow of your breathing to stabilize your brain.
    </p>
